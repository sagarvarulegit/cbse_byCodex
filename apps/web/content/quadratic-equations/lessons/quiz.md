# Quadratic Equations chapter quiz

## 1. Standard form

Which is the standard form of a quadratic equation?

1. $ax+b=0$
2. $ax^2+bx+c=0$, $a\ne0$
3. $ax^3+bx+c=0$
4. $a/x+b=0$

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 4.2 aligned
Explanation: A quadratic has highest non-zero power $2$.

## 2. Factorisation roots

The roots of $x^2-5x+6=0$ are:

1. $-2,-3$
2. $1,6$
3. $2,3$
4. $-1,-6$

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 4.3 aligned
Explanation: $(x-2)(x-3)=0$.

## 3. Discriminant

For $3x^2-4x+2=0$, the discriminant is:

1. $8$
2. $-8$
3. $16$
4. $24$

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 4.4 aligned
Explanation: $D=(-4)^2-4(3)(2)=16-24=-8$.

## 4. Nature of roots

If $D=0$, a quadratic equation has:

1. no real roots
2. two distinct real roots
3. two equal real roots
4. infinitely many roots

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 4.4 aligned
Explanation: Both formula values become $-b/(2a)$.

## 5. Formula sign

The correct quadratic formula is:

1. $(-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac})/(2a)$
2. $(b\pm\sqrt{b^2+4ac})/(2a)$
3. $(-b\pm\sqrt{b^2}-4ac)/(2a)$
4. $(-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac})/a$

Answer: 1
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Section 4.4 aligned
Explanation: The entire discriminant stays under the square root and the denominator is $2a$.

## 6. Equal-root parameter

If $2x^2+kx+8=0$ has equal roots, then:

1. $k=0$
2. $k=8$ only
3. $k=-8$ only
4. $k=\pm8$

Answer: 4
Topic: ncert-exercises-and-revision
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Exercise 4.3 aligned
Explanation: $D=k^2-64=0$.

## 7. Contextual root

A length problem gives roots $12$ and $-12.5$. Which root is admissible?

1. both
2. $12$ only
3. $-12.5$ only
4. neither

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Example 6 aligned
Explanation: A physical length cannot be negative.

## 8. Product of consecutive integers

Two consecutive positive integers have product $306$. They are:

1. $16,17$
2. $17,18$
3. $18,19$
4. $19,20$

Answer: 2
Topic: ncert-exercises-and-revision
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Exercise 4.1 aligned
Explanation: $17\times18=306$.
