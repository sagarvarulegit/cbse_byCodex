# Nutrition and Diet chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Balanced diet

A balanced diet provides:

1. nutrients in proper amounts
2. only sugar
3. no water

Answer: 1
Explanation: A balanced diet gives the body the right nutrients in proper amounts.

## 2. Protein

Protein mainly helps in:

1. growth and repair
2. only eye colour
3. only digestion of metals

Answer: 1
Explanation: Protein supports growth and repair of body tissues.

## 3. Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates mainly provide:

1. energy
2. no calories
3. oxygen

Answer: 1
Explanation: Carbohydrates are the body's main source of energy.

## 4. Vitamins

Vitamins help:

1. body functions and immunity
2. make iron rods
3. stop growth

Answer: 1
Explanation: Vitamins support body functions and help immunity.

## 5. Hydration

Water is important for:

1. body functions and temperature balance
2. replacing all food
3. making bones disappear

Answer: 1
Explanation: Water helps body functions and keeps temperature balanced.
