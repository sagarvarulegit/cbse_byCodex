# Sports and Games chapter quiz

## 1. Sportsmanship

Sportsmanship means:

1. fair play and respect
2. cheating to win
3. avoiding rules

Answer: 1
Explanation: Sportsmanship means fair play and respect for others.

## 2. Teamwork

Team games teach:

1. cooperation
2. isolation only
3. no communication

Answer: 1
Explanation: Team games build cooperation and shared effort.

## 3. Rules

Rules in games help:

1. make play fair and safe
2. create confusion only
3. remove discipline

Answer: 1
Explanation: Rules keep games fair and safe.

## 4. Practice

Regular practice improves:

1. skill and confidence
2. only laziness
3. no ability

Answer: 1
Explanation: Practice builds skill and confidence.

## 5. Health

Sports support:

1. fitness and mental well-being
2. inactivity
3. poor habits only

Answer: 1
Explanation: Sports support physical fitness and mental well-being.
