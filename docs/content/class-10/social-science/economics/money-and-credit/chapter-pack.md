# Money and Credit

- explain self-help groups

## 1) money as a medium of exchange

Money makes exchange easier because people can buy and sell without needing double coincidence of wants.

In barter, both sides must want what the other offers. Money removes this problem.

## 2) modern forms of money

Modern money includes currency and deposits with banks.

Currency is accepted because it is authorised by the government and the central bank.

Demand deposits can be withdrawn on demand and used through cheques, cards, and digital transfers.

## 3) deposits with banks

People deposit extra money in banks for safety and interest.

Banks keep a small portion as cash and use the rest to give loans.

## 4) loan activities of banks

Banks mediate between depositors and borrowers.

They pay interest on deposits and charge higher interest on loans. The difference supports bank operations.

## 5) credit situations

Credit can be helpful when it raises income, supports production, or handles emergencies.

Credit can become harmful when borrowers cannot repay and fall into a debt trap.

## 6) terms of credit

Terms of credit include interest rate, collateral, documentation, mode of repayment, and loan period.

Collateral is an asset pledged by the borrower, which the lender can sell if the loan is not repaid.

## 7) formal and informal credit

Formal sources include banks and cooperatives. They are supervised by the Reserve Bank of India and usually charge lower interest.

Informal sources include moneylenders, traders, employers, relatives, and friends. They may charge high interest and use unfair conditions.

Affordable formal credit is important for development.

## 8) self-help groups

Self-help groups are small groups, often of rural women, who save regularly and give loans to members.

They help members access credit without heavy collateral and build confidence, cooperation, and financial discipline.

## exam-friendly summary notes

- Money solves double coincidence of wants.
- Currency and demand deposits are modern money.
- Banks use deposits to give loans.
- Terms of credit include interest, collateral, documents, and repayment.
- Formal credit is usually cheaper and regulated.
- SHGs help poor borrowers access credit.

## common mistakes students make

1. Defining money only as coins and notes.
2. Forgetting demand deposits as money.
3. Writing credit is always good.
4. Confusing collateral with interest.
5. Ignoring RBI supervision of formal credit.

## board tips

- In credit answers, show both positive and negative outcomes.
- Use the term "double coincidence of wants" for barter.
- For formal/informal comparison, mention regulation and interest rate.
