# Words and Expressions Unit 5 - Glimpses of India

## chapter goal

By the end of this workbook unit, a student should be able to:
- research and present cultural diversity across Indian states
- read passages on communities, food, and memory
- build vocabulary for culture and travel
- practise grammar, editing, speaking, writing, and project work

## teacher note on textbook alignment

This pack follows `jewe205.pdf`: group work on Indian states, regional cuisine, dance, music, art, map marking, reading comprehension on community and food, vocabulary, grammar, editing, speaking, writing, and project work.

## chapter flow for the platform

1. Cultural research task
2. Map and presentation work
3. Reading comprehension
4. Vocabulary of culture
5. Grammar and editing
6. Speaking and writing
7. Project work

## 1) research task

The unit asks students to collect information about Indian states.

Focus areas include cuisine, dance, music, art, and interesting cultural facts.

## 2) map and presentation

Map marking connects language learning with geography and culture.

A good presentation should be brief, accurate, and visually organised.

## 3) reading skill

The reading passage connects food with community memory and identity.

Read for details, sequence, people, place, and cultural meaning.

## 4) vocabulary

Vocabulary fields include food, hospitality, tradition, migration, community, and celebration.

Use words in sentences connected to culture.

## 5) writing

Writing may include descriptive paragraphs, travel notes, reports, or presentations.

Use sensory details: taste, smell, sound, colour, and place.

## exam-friendly summary notes

- The unit extends "Glimpses of India" into cultural research.
- Use accurate state-wise details.
- Reading passages may connect food with identity.
- Presentations need structure and clarity.
- Descriptive writing should use sensory details.

## common mistakes students make

1. Listing facts without organising them.
2. Mixing state, language, and art details inaccurately.
3. Making presentations too long.

## board tips

- Use table format for research notes.
- Verify spellings of place names and art forms.
- In descriptive writing, include specific details.
