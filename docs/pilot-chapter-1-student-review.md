# Chapter 1 Small Student Review

Use this record for a controlled review with 2-5 Class 10 students. Give each student 20-30 minutes. Use participant codes such as `S01`; do not record names, phone numbers, email addresses, or school identifiers.

## Review goal

Find the single highest-friction problem in *Chemical Reactions and Equations* before widening the pilot.

Observe:

- whether the student understands the first lesson without coaching
- whether navigation and practice shortcuts are clear
- whether numbered figure pins and the matching label key are readable
- whether quiz wording, options, and feedback support learning

## Facilitator rules

- Let the student use the platform naturally.
- Do not explain chemistry unless the student is completely blocked.
- Record every hint or rescue given.
- Ask the comprehension questions only after the student has studied the relevant section.
- Record what happened, not what you expected to happen.
- Do not promise that every suggestion will be implemented.

## Student tasks

1. Open *Chemical Reactions and Equations* and decide where to begin.
2. Read the first lesson through Activity 1.3.
3. Use Figure 1.1 or Figure 1.2 to identify two labelled parts.
4. Use **Jump to practice** and answer one practice prompt aloud.
5. Explain two observations that can indicate a chemical reaction.
6. Explain why a chemical formula must not be changed while balancing an equation.
7. Open the chapter quiz and answer at least five questions.
8. Submit one short feedback message from the surface that caused the most difficulty.
