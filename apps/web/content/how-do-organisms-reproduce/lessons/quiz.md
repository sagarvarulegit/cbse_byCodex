# How do Organisms Reproduce? chapter quiz

## 1. DNA copying

Why is DNA copying essential in reproduction?

1. it transfers hereditary information
2. it prevents all variation
3. it replaces nutrition

Answer: 1
Topic: dna-copying-and-variation
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT in-text
Explanation: DNA carries the instructions that preserve the species' body design.

## 2. Variation

Variation is especially useful because it:

1. guarantees every individual will live longer
2. may help some members survive environmental change
3. stops evolution

Answer: 2
Topic: dna-copying-and-variation
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT in-text
Explanation: A favourable variant may maintain the species when its niche changes.

## 3. Amoeba

Amoeba reproduces asexually mainly by:

1. binary fission in any plane
2. longitudinal fission only
3. multiple fission only

Answer: 1
Topic: fission-fragmentation-regeneration-and-budding
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Figure 7.1
Explanation: Amoeba lacks a fixed body axis, so division may occur in any plane.

## 4. Plasmodium

Plasmodium produces many daughter cells at once by:

1. budding
2. multiple fission
3. fragmentation

Answer: 2
Topic: fission-fragmentation-regeneration-and-budding
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Figure 7.2
Explanation: Multiple fission releases many daughter cells simultaneously.

## 5. Spirogyra

The usual asexual mode shown by Spirogyra is:

1. fragmentation
2. budding
3. regeneration

Answer: 1
Topic: fission-fragmentation-regeneration-and-budding
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Activity 7.4
Explanation: Mature filaments break into pieces, each of which grows into a new filament.

## 6. Hydra

Which two processes are correctly linked with Hydra?

1. budding and regeneration
2. fragmentation and binary fission
3. multiple fission and spore formation

Answer: 1
Topic: fission-fragmentation-regeneration-and-budding
Difficulty: medium
Source: CBSE 2023 five-mark family
Explanation: Hydra forms reproductive buds and can regenerate missing parts after cutting.

## 7. Potato

Which potato piece is most likely to sprout?

1. a piece containing an eye or bud
2. any peel with no bud
3. a dry piece with no living tissue

Answer: 1
Topic: vegetative-propagation-and-spore-formation
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Activity 7.5
Explanation: A potato eye contains the bud needed to produce a new shoot.

## 8. Rhizopus

Spores of Rhizopus are produced inside:

1. sporangia
2. cotyledons
3. ovules

Answer: 1
Topic: vegetative-propagation-and-spore-formation
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Figure 7.6
Explanation: Round sporangia contain many thick-walled spores.

## 9. Meiosis

What prevents chromosome number from doubling every sexual generation?

1. meiosis halves it in gametes
2. fertilisation destroys half the chromosomes
3. the zygote contains no DNA

Answer: 1
Topic: why-sexual-reproduction
Difficulty: medium
Source: CBSE 2025-2026 board family
Explanation: Two half sets combine during fertilisation to restore the full number.

## 10. Anther

The anther contains:

1. ovules
2. pollen grains
3. embryos

Answer: 2
Topic: sexual-reproduction-in-flowering-plants
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Exercise 3
Explanation: The anther is the pollen-producing part of a stamen.

## 11. Pollination

Pollination is:

1. fusion of gametes inside an ovule
2. transfer of pollen from anther to stigma
3. conversion of ovary into fruit

Answer: 2
Topic: sexual-reproduction-in-flowering-plants
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT in-text
Explanation: Fertilisation occurs later, after pollen-tube growth.

## 12. Post-fertilisation

Which pair is correct?

1. ovule -> fruit; ovary -> seed
2. ovule -> seed; ovary -> fruit
3. stigma -> seed; anther -> fruit

Answer: 2
Topic: sexual-reproduction-in-flowering-plants
Difficulty: medium
Source: CBSE 2023-2026 board family
Explanation: The embryo develops inside the seed formed from the ovule.

## 13. Germination

Which seed part forms the future root?

1. plumule
2. radicle
3. cotyledon

Answer: 2
Topic: sexual-reproduction-in-flowering-plants
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Figure 7.9
Explanation: The plumule forms the shoot and the radicle forms the root.

## 14. Testes

Why are the testes located in the scrotum?

1. sperm formation needs a temperature below normal body temperature
2. fertilisation occurs there
3. they store urine

Answer: 1
Topic: puberty-and-male-reproductive-system
Difficulty: medium
Source: CBSE 2023-2026 board family
Explanation: The external scrotal location provides a cooler environment.

## 15. Seminal glands

Seminal vesicles and the prostate mainly:

1. produce eggs
2. add nutritive transport fluid to sperms
3. cause menstruation

Answer: 2
Topic: puberty-and-male-reproductive-system
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT in-text
Explanation: Their secretions support and transport sperms as semen.

## 16. Fertilisation site

Human fertilisation usually occurs in the:

1. uterus
2. oviduct
3. vagina

Answer: 2
Topic: female-reproductive-system-fertilisation-and-menstruation
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT/CBSE recurring
Explanation: Implantation, not fertilisation, occurs in the uterus.

## 17. Placenta

Which statement about placenta is correct?

1. it provides exchange of nutrients, oxygen, and wastes
2. it produces pollen
3. it is the site of sperm formation

Answer: 1
Topic: female-reproductive-system-fertilisation-and-menstruation
Difficulty: medium
Source: CBSE recurring
Explanation: Embryonic villi and maternal blood spaces provide a large exchange surface.

## 18. Menstruation

Menstruation occurs when:

1. an embryo implants
2. the egg is not fertilised and the prepared uterine lining breaks down
3. a sperm enters the oviduct

Answer: 2
Topic: female-reproductive-system-fertilisation-and-menstruation
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Exercise 6
Explanation: The unused blood-rich lining is discharged as blood and mucus.

## 19. Copper-T

Which statement is correct?

1. Copper-T prevents pregnancy but does not protect against STIs
2. Copper-T blocks the vas deferens
3. Copper-T is a barrier worn on the penis

Answer: 1
Topic: reproductive-health-and-contraception
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT in-text
Explanation: Copper-T is placed in the uterus and is not an STI barrier.

## 20. Surgical contraception

Tubectomy prevents pregnancy by blocking the:

1. urethra
2. fallopian tubes
3. seminal vesicles

Answer: 2
Topic: reproductive-health-and-contraception
Difficulty: medium
Source: CBSE 2026 official scheme
Explanation: Vasectomy blocks the vas deferens; tubectomy blocks the fallopian tubes.
