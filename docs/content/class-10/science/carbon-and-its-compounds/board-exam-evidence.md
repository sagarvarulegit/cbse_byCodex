organic-chemistry marking point, and unrelated natural-acid MCQs were removed
from the coverage decision.

`C3H6`, name/draw propene, connect it to propanol dehydration and hydrogenate

`09-board-exam-practice.md`.

explicitly taught, answered in the NCERT resource and sampled in board practice
