# The Age of Industrialisation chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Industrialisation

Industrialisation refers to:

1. growth of machine-based production and factories
2. only forest conservation
3. only political parties

Answer: 1
Explanation: Industrialisation means growth of machine-based production and factories.

## 2. Proto-industrialisation

Proto-industrialisation means:

1. production before factories became dominant
2. no production at all
3. only modern software

Answer: 1
Explanation: Proto-industrialisation refers to production before factory dominance.

## 3. Labour

Industrialisation changed:

1. work, labour, and urban life
2. only mountain height
3. only rainfall

Answer: 1
Explanation: Industrialisation changed work, labour, and city life.

## 4. Indian textiles

Indian textile producers faced pressure from:

1. machine-made British goods
2. no competition
3. only local festivals

Answer: 1
Explanation: Machine-made British textiles created strong competition.

## 5. Advertisements

Labels and advertisements helped:

1. sell industrial goods
2. stop all trade
3. remove factories

Answer: 1
Explanation: Labels and advertisements helped sell industrial products.
