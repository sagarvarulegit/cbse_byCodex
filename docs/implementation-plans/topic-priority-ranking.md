# CBSE Class 10 Topic Priority Ranking

Research date: 2026-08-08 · Purpose: decide which board chapters to produce YouTube videos for next.

## Methodology

- **Sources consulted** (full list with URLs in the [Sources](#sources) section):
  - *Official / primary:* CBSE 2025-26 Class X sample question papers (SQP) and marking schemes for Maths, Science and Social Science (`cbseacademic.nic.in`), CBSE Class X Mathematics curriculum 2026-27 PDF, CBSE Class X Science syllabus 2024-25 PDF (whose assessment scheme CBSE states continues into 2025-26), CBSE curriculum index 2025-26.
  - *Secondary:* Physics Wallah chapter-wise weightage pages for Maths, Science and Social Science. Educart, Oswaal, Vedantu, Selfstudys and LearnCBSE weightage/PYQ-analysis pages were attempted but were unreachable at research time (404 / bot captcha) — they are listed as consulted but unavailable; **no claim in this document rests on them**.
  - *Local project archives (previously verified):* `docs/content/class-10/science/remaining-chapters-board-coverage-audit.md` (CBSE Class 10 Science archive, 165 papers 2022–2026), `docs/content/class-10/science/*/board-exam-evidence.md` (PYQs visually verified against original paper pages + official marking schemes 2023–2026), and the two video source maps under `videos/`.
- **Data period:** board sessions 2020–2026. Two sessions in this window are non-standard and counted separately: **2021** (COVID, ~30% syllabus, MCQ-only) and **2022** (two separate Term-1/Term-2 papers). "Every year" below means every normal session (2020, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026), consistent with the 2025-26 SQP and the project's 2022–2026 Science archive.
- **Ranking criteria:** marks weightage 50% + frequency of appearance 50%. For Mathematics and Social Science, unit/book-level marks are official (CBSE publishes no chapter-level split), so chapter marks are **estimates** derived from secondary distributions + the official 2025-26 SQP question-to-chapter mapping; for Science, chapter presence is verified from the local 2022–2026 paper archive, while per-chapter marks are estimates inside official unit totals.
- **Confidence labels:** [OFFICIAL] = from a fetched CBSE document; [VERIFIED] = visually verified by the project against original paper/marking-scheme pages; [EST] = estimate/inference, explicitly not an official CBSE number.
- **Paper structure note:** the question-count/section layouts below are the **official 2025-26 SQP** (a practice paper) — the active Maths curriculum PDF deliberately publishes *no* fixed question count, so the SQP structure is a design guide, not a binding rule.

## Top 10 Priority Topics (Recommended for Video Production)

### Rank 1: Life Processes
- Subject: Science
- Chapter: NCERT Ch 5, Life Processes (Unit II – World of Living, 25 marks) [OFFICIAL]
- Marks weightage: ~8–10 marks per paper (est. share of the 25-mark unit; the largest biology chapter) [EST]
- Frequency: appeared in every archived year 2022–2026 [VERIFIED — local archive]; present in the official 2025-26 SQP (4-mark case study on digestion + MCQs) [OFFICIAL]; near-every-year historically
- Common question types: MCQ, 2-mark, 3-mark, 5-mark, case-based (digestion/respiration/transport), diagram labelling
- Why important: top chapter of the highest biology unit; guaranteed presence; highly visual (great for animation); platform notes already exist to source the script
- Video priority: **HIGH**

### Rank 2: Chemical Reactions and Equations
- Subject: Science
- Chapter: NCERT Ch 1, Chemical Reactions and Equations (Unit I – Chemical Substances – Nature & Behaviour, 25 marks) [OFFICIAL]
- Marks weightage: ~7–9 marks per paper (est. share of the 25-mark unit) [EST]
- Frequency: PYQs visually verified in **2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026** board papers (official marking schemes) [VERIFIED]; present in official 2025-26 SQP (balancing/redox/displacement items) [OFFICIAL]
- Common question types: MCQ, Assertion-Reason, 2-mark, 3-mark, 5-mark, case-based (balancing, observations, redox)
- Why important: every-year verified presence; the platform's gold-standard chapter already has 2023–2026 PYQ answer guides to reuse; reactions are very animatable
- Video priority: **HIGH**

### Rank 3: Electricity
- Subject: Science
- Chapter: NCERT Ch 11, Electricity (Unit IV – Effects of Electric Current, 13 marks) [OFFICIAL]
- Marks weightage: ~7–8 marks per paper (est. share; the dominant chapter of its unit) [EST]
- Frequency: every archived year 2022–2026 [VERIFIED]; official 2025-26 SQP carries a **5-mark** numerical (resistors/power) and a 3-mark question [OFFICIAL]
- Common question types: MCQ, 3-mark, **5-mark numericals**, case-based circuits
- Why important: the guaranteed numerical-5-marker chapter in physics; formula + circuit diagrams animate well
- Video priority: **HIGH**

### Rank 4: Light – Reflection and Refraction
- Subject: Science
- Chapter: NCERT Ch 9, Light – Reflection and Refraction (Unit III – Natural Phenomena, 12 marks) [OFFICIAL]
- Marks weightage: ~6–8 marks per paper (est. share of the unit) [EST]
- Frequency: every archived year 2022–2026 [VERIFIED]; official 2025-26 SQP includes a **5-mark** lens/focal-length question [OFFICIAL]
- Common question types: MCQ, 3-mark, **5-mark mirror/lens numericals**, case-based
- Why important: second guaranteed numerical-5-marker; ray diagrams are among the most visual content in the syllabus
- Video priority: **HIGH**

### Rank 5: Quadratic Equations
- Subject: Math
- Chapter: NCERT Ch 4, Quadratic Equations (Unit II – Algebra, 20 marks — largest unit) [OFFICIAL]
- Marks weightage: ~6–8 marks per paper (est.) [EST]
- Frequency: appeared every year (2020–2026, incl. 2025-26 SQP) [EST + OFFICIAL SQP]; the chapter-level "5-mark word problem" is a near-annual pattern [EST]
- Common question types: 2-mark, 3-mark, **5-mark word problems** (speed/age/area), MCQ
- Why important: guaranteed 5-mark territory in the single highest-weightage unit; step-by-step solving videos have proven search demand
- Video priority: **HIGH**

### Rank 6: Triangles
- Subject: Math
- Chapter: NCERT Ch 6, Triangles (Unit IV – Geometry, 15 marks) [OFFICIAL]
- Marks weightage: ~8–10 marks per paper (est.; the biggest geometry chapter) [EST]
- Frequency: every year (2020–2026, incl. 2025-26 SQP) [EST + OFFICIAL SQP]
- Common question types: 3-mark and **5-mark similarity/BPT proofs**, MCQ
- Why important: highest estimated per-chapter marks in Maths; proof-writing videos are high-value and reusable
- Video priority: **HIGH**

### Rank 7: Arithmetic Progressions
- Subject: Math
- Chapter: NCERT Ch 5, Arithmetic Progressions (Unit II – Algebra, 20 marks) [OFFICIAL]
- Marks weightage: ~5–7 marks per paper (est.) [EST]
- Frequency: every year (2020–2026, incl. 2025-26 SQP) [EST + OFFICIAL SQP]
- Common question types: 2-mark, 3-mark, **5-mark** (sum of n terms, word problems)
- Why important: consistent 5-mark appearances; formula-pattern videos (an = a+(n−1)d, Sn) are compact and high-utility
- Video priority: **HIGH**

### Rank 8: Nationalism in India
- Subject: Social Science
- Chapter: History – India and the Contemporary World II, Ch 2 Nationalism in India (History section = 20 marks) [OFFICIAL]
- Marks weightage: ~8–10 marks per paper (est. from secondary distributions — History rows on secondary pages do not sum cleanly to 20, so treat as rough) [EST]
- Frequency: every year (2020–2026, incl. 2025-26 SQP which pairs it with a picture MCQ + 3-mark SA) [EST + OFFICIAL SQP]
- Common question types: 1-mark picture MCQ, 3-mark SA, **5-mark LA**, 1 map mark
- Why important: the single most-asked SS chapter; timeline/map storytelling suits video
- Video priority: **HIGH**

### Rank 9: Introduction to Trigonometry
- Subject: Math
- Chapter: NCERT Ch 8, Introduction to Trigonometry (Unit V – Trigonometry, 12 marks) [OFFICIAL]
- Marks weightage: ~6–8 marks per paper (est.) [EST]
- Frequency: every year (2020–2026; official 2025-26 SQP has two MCQs here) [EST + OFFICIAL SQP]
- Common question types: MCQ, 3-mark identity proofs; pairs with Ch 9 Applications of Trigonometry (case study / heights & distances — also in 2025-26 SQP)
- Why important: identity-based marks are predictable and drill-friendly; its companion chapter Ch 9 is a case-study favourite
- Video priority: **HIGH**

### Rank 10: Statistics & Probability
- Subject: Math
- Chapter: NCERT Ch 13 Statistics + Ch 14 Probability (Unit VII – Statistics & Probability, 11 marks) [OFFICIAL]
- Marks weightage: ~4–6 marks each per paper (est.) [EST]
- Frequency: every year; official 2025-26 SQP carries MCQs in both (mean/median/mode; basic probability) [EST + OFFICIAL SQP]
- Common question types: MCQ, 3-mark (mean/median/mode, probability problems)
- Why important: steady combined unit weight (11 marks); low-difficulty marks that students can secure — good "easy win" content
- Video priority: **HIGH**

## Recommendation

**Build the next video on Life Processes (Science Ch 5).** It scores highest on both criteria:
1. **Marks:** it is the largest chapter in the 25-mark World of Living unit (~8–10 est. marks) — the single biggest chapter-level share of any unit in the three subjects.
2. **Frequency:** verified in every archived year 2022–2026 (including a 4-mark case study and MCQs in the official 2025-26 SQP) — an every-year lock.

It is also the best *production* fit: nutrition/respiration/transport/excretion are inherently visual (diagram-driven), and the platform already has six lesson-wise Life Processes notes plus a board-evidence file to source the script from — the same pipeline used for the SAV video.

**If the channel should stay in Maths** (the previous two videos were Maths — marks-plan + SAV), the Maths pick is **Quadratic Equations (Ch 4)**: guaranteed 5-mark word problems, Algebra is the highest-weightage unit (20 marks), and the solving-method format matches the existing video style.

**Follow-ups after the top-4 Science run:** Electricity → Light (both guaranteed 5-mark numericals), then Nationalism in India as the first SS video.

**Source-map rule for the video itself** (per our own video standards): the video must not claim "most important chapter" as an official CBSE statement, must not promise guaranteed/repeated marks, and chapter-level mark numbers must be voiced as estimates ("roughly X marks, not an official CBSE figure"). All claims go into the video's `SOURCE_MAP.md` in the same format as `videos/cbse-class-10-maths-sav/SOURCE_MAP.md` before narration is finalised. Re-check the official Class X SQP/curriculum pages again when the 2026-27 main-subject SQPs are published.
