# Our Environment chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Ecosystem

An ecosystem includes:

1. living and non-living things together
2. only animals
3. only water

Answer: 1
Explanation: An ecosystem includes both living and non-living parts together.

## 2. Food chain

A food chain shows:

1. transfer of energy
2. transfer of electricity
3. transfer of sound only

Answer: 1
Explanation: A food chain shows how energy passes from one organism to another.

## 3. Trophic level

Each step in a food chain is a:

1. trophic level
2. mineral layer
3. weather system

Answer: 1
Explanation: Each position in a food chain is a trophic level.

## 4. Biodegradable

Biodegradable waste:

1. can be broken down by microorganisms
2. cannot change at all
3. is always metal

Answer: 1
Explanation: Biodegradable waste can be broken down by microbes.

## 5. Ozone

The ozone layer helps protect us from:

1. harmful ultraviolet rays
2. rainwater
3. sound waves

Answer: 1
Explanation: The ozone layer blocks harmful ultraviolet rays.
