## Extraction limitations

The extracted dataset was used only to locate possible source pages. It is not
safe as a publication source by itself. During this audit it:

- merged adjacent questions and OR choices;
- damaged subscripts, arrows and state symbols;
- sometimes assigned the wrong printed page or discipline;
- repeated set variants;
- produced a false Chapter 2 hit for 2025 paper `31/5/1`, Question 34, even
  though the rendered original page showed a Carbon and Its Compounds question.

Publication rule: a question receives a **PYQ** label only after the original
English paper page and the matching official marking-scheme page have both been
rendered and visually checked.

## Marking-point audit result

The 11 historical answers and the latest official SQP answer include every
separately awarded value point found in the matching official schemes:

- direct identification before explanation;
- balanced equations and conditions;
- exact anode/cathode gases where asked;
- observations as well as inference;
- parent acid and parent base for salt classification;
- pH thresholds `7.0-7.8`, `<5.6`, and tooth-enamel risk below `5.5`;
- correct strong/weak ion reasoning;
- named uses in the quantity requested;
- explicit statement that soluble sodium sulphate means no precipitate;
- safe dilution rule: add acid to water slowly with stirring, never water to
  concentrated acid.

## Coverage decision

The Chapter 2 concept path now covers every demand family found in the
2023-2026 English discovery screen. The board-practice supplement supplies
write-before-check practice for the highest-value integrated demands and the
current SQP case format.

This is a **source-and-marking-point coverage result**, not a guarantee of a
particular future paper. A final chapter-quality claim still requires the
technical, visual, learner-transfer and retention gates defined by the gold
standard.

## Reuse rule for Chapters 3-13

1. Finish the rendered current-textbook map before searching board papers.
2. Use extraction only for discovery.
3. Render every quoted original paper page.
4. Check the matching official marking-scheme page.
5. Preserve year, paper code, question number and printed page.
6. Publish verified PYQs separately from original board-style questions.
7. Map every discovered demand family to a teaching and practice destination.
8. Repair missing marking points in the lesson before calling the family
   covered.
