## Audit scope

Its structured extraction contains 3,266 question instances and 2,880
deduplicated questions. A guarded Chapter 3 keyword screen produced 120 strong
instances:

## Normalisation and false-positive controls

- Questions were classified by complete demand, not by a single keyword.
- Cross-chapter chemistry questions were retained only when Chapter 3 knowledge
  was required for a marking point.
- Repeated regional sets were used to confirm demand frequency but were not
  mistaken for new concepts.
- One 2025 marking-scheme rendering appears to abbreviate copper(I) sulphide as
  `CuS`. The platform follows the NCERT reaction and chemically correct formula
  `Cu2S`.

## Coverage conclusion

**Pass for the audited 2022-2026 archive and the official 2025-26 sample
paper.** Every distinct Chapter 3 demand family found in the archive is taught
in the canonical lessons, answered in the NCERT question/exercise lesson, and
sampled in either the board-practice lesson or quiz. The student-facing board
practice includes marking points, balanced equations, diagram labels and
OR-choice completion checks, so the notes contain the knowledge needed to
answer the audited questions without an external guide.
