# Triangles chapter quiz

## 1. Similar figures

Two polygons are similar when their corresponding angles are equal and their corresponding sides are:

1. equal only
2. perpendicular
3. proportional
4. parallel

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 6.2 aligned
Explanation: Equal angles and one common scale factor preserve the shape.

## 2. Basic proportionality theorem

In $\triangle ABC$, $DE\parallel BC$ with $D$ on $AB$ and $E$ on $AC$. Then:

1. $AD/DB=AE/EC$
2. $AD/AB=EC/AC$
3. $AB/DB=AC/AE$
4. $AD=AE$

Answer: 1
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 6.3 aligned
Explanation: A line parallel to one side divides the other two sides in the same ratio.

## 3. Converse of BPT

If a line cuts two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line is:

1. perpendicular to the third side
2. parallel to the third side
3. equal to the third side
4. a median

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 6.3 aligned
Explanation: This is the converse of the Basic Proportionality Theorem.

## 4. AA similarity

If two angles of one triangle equal two corresponding angles of another triangle, the triangles are similar by:

1. SSS
2. SAS
3. AA
4. RHS

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 6.4 aligned
Explanation: Equality of two angle pairs forces equality of the third pair as well.

## 5. SSS similarity

Triangles with side lengths $3,4,5$ and $6,8,10$ are:

1. congruent
2. similar by SSS
3. similar by AA only
4. not similar

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 6.4 aligned
Explanation: All three corresponding side ratios are $1:2$.

## 6. SAS similarity

For SAS similarity, the equal angle must lie:

1. opposite the longest side
2. between the two proportional side pairs
3. outside both triangles
4. anywhere in each triangle

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Section 6.4 aligned
Explanation: SAS uses two proportional sides and their included equal angle.

## 7. Writing correspondence

If $\triangle ABC\sim\triangle PQR$, which correspondence is correct?

1. $A\leftrightarrow Q, B\leftrightarrow P, C\leftrightarrow R$
2. $A\leftrightarrow P, B\leftrightarrow Q, C\leftrightarrow R$
3. $A\leftrightarrow R, B\leftrightarrow Q, C\leftrightarrow P$
4. Correspondence cannot be read from the order

Answer: 2
Topic: ncert-exercises-and-revision
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Exercise 6.3 aligned
Explanation: The written order names corresponding vertices in matching positions.

## 8. Choosing a criterion

Two sides of one triangle are proportional to two sides of another, and the included angles are equal. Which criterion proves similarity?

1. AA
2. SSS
3. SAS
4. BPT

Answer: 3
Topic: ncert-exercises-and-revision
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Exercise 6.3 aligned
Explanation: These are precisely the hypotheses of the SAS similarity criterion.
