# Surface Areas and Volumes chapter quiz

## 1. Joined solids

When two solid faces are joined completely, the common face is:

1. counted twice in exposed surface area
2. counted once in exposed surface area
3. not counted in exposed surface area
4. added to the volume

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 12.2 aligned
Explanation: The joined face becomes internal and is no longer exposed.

## 2. Cylinder formula

The curved surface area of a cylinder is:

1. $\pi r^2h$
2. $2\pi rh$
3. $2\pi r(r+h)$
4. $\pi rl$

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 12.2 prerequisite aligned
Explanation: Unrolling the curved surface gives a rectangle of dimensions $2\pi r$ and $h$.

## 3. Volume of combined solids

For a solid made by joining a cone and a hemisphere without overlap, total volume is:

1. cone volume minus hemisphere volume
2. cone volume plus hemisphere volume
3. only the larger volume
4. their surface areas added

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 12.3 aligned
Explanation: Non-overlapping material volumes are additive.

## 4. Hollow object

The volume of material in a hollow cylinder is found by:

1. adding outer and inner cylinder volumes
2. subtracting inner volume from outer volume
3. using curved surface area only
4. multiplying both radii

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 12.3 aligned
Explanation: The empty cavity must be removed from the enclosing solid.

## 5. Depression in a solid

A hemispherical depression is cut from the top of a cube. For exposed surface area, the circular opening is replaced by:

1. nothing
2. the curved surface of the hemisphere
3. a second square face
4. the volume of the hemisphere

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Section 12.2 aligned
Explanation: Subtract the covered circular patch and add the newly exposed curved depression.

## 6. Converting capacity

$1$ litre equals:

1. $1$ cm$^3$
2. $10$ cm$^3$
3. $100$ cm$^3$
4. $1000$ cm$^3$

Answer: 4
Topic: ncert-exercises-and-revision
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Exercise 12.2 aligned
Explanation: Capacity problems often require $1\text{ L}=1000\text{ cm}^3$.

## 8. Surface area or volume

A question asks how much paint is needed for a toy. The relevant quantity is usually:

1. exposed surface area
2. volume
3. diameter only
4. density only

Answer: 1
Topic: ncert-exercises-and-revision
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Exercise 12.1 aligned
Explanation: Paint covers exposed surfaces rather than filling the solid.
