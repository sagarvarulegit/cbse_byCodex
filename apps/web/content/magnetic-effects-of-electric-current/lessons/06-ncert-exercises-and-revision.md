## 7. Direction rules

(i) Straight-conductor magnetic field: right-hand thumb rule—thumb current,
curled fingers field.

(ii) Force on perpendicular current: Fleming left-hand rule—forefinger field,
middle current, thumb force.

(iii) Induced current from rotating coil: Fleming right-hand rule—right
forefinger field, thumb conductor motion, middle induced current.

## 8. Short circuit

Live and neutral come into direct contact, making resistance very low and
current abruptly very high.

## 9. Earth wire

It connects a metallic body to earth by a low-resistance path, keeps the body
near earth potential, and enables rapid protective disconnection during
leakage, preventing severe shock.
