# Control and Coordination chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Receptor

Which part detects a stimulus first?

1. receptor
2. synapse
3. cerebellum

Answer: 1
Topic: animal-nervous-system
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT in-text aligned
Explanation: Receptors in sense organs detect the stimulus and start the message.

## 2. Synapse

The gap between two neurons is called a:

1. dendrite
2. synapse
3. axon

Answer: 2
Topic: animal-nervous-system
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT exercise aligned
Explanation: The synapse is the gap where chemicals pass the signal from one neuron to the next.

## 3. Reflex

A reflex action is:

1. a slow planned action
2. a quick automatic response
3. a type of digestion

Answer: 2
Topic: voluntary-involuntary-reflex-actions
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT in-text aligned
Explanation: Reflex actions are fast, automatic responses to a stimulus.

## 4. Brain

Which part of the brain maintains posture and balance?

3. cerebellum

Answer: 3
Topic: voluntary-involuntary-reflex-actions
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT in-text aligned
Explanation: The cerebellum controls posture, balance, and precision of voluntary actions.

## 6. Phototropism
