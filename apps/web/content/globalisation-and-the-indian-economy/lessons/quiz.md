# Globalisation and the Indian Economy chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Globalisation

Globalisation means:

1. integration of markets and economies
2. ending all trade
3. only village production

Answer: 1
Explanation: Globalisation connects markets and economies across borders.

## 2. MNC

An MNC is a company that:

1. operates in more than one country
2. operates only in one classroom
3. never invests

Answer: 1
Explanation: An MNC works in more than one country.

## 3. Technology

Globalisation has been helped by:

1. transport and communication technology
2. no communication
3. banning trade

Answer: 1
Explanation: Transport and communication technology make global business easier.

## 4. WTO

WTO is linked with:

1. international trade rules
2. local school exams
3. monsoon rainfall

Answer: 1
Explanation: The WTO deals with rules for international trade.

## 5. Fair globalisation

Fair globalisation means:

1. benefits should reach workers, consumers, and producers fairly
2. only big companies matter
3. workers should lose rights

Answer: 1
Explanation: Fair globalisation shares benefits fairly instead of favouring only large companies.
