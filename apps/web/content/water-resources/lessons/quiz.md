# Water Resources chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Scarcity

Water scarcity means:

1. shortage of usable water
2. excess of every resource
3. no need for conservation

Answer: 1
Explanation: Water scarcity means there is not enough usable water for the need.

## 2. Dams

Multipurpose river projects can help with:

1. irrigation and electricity
2. only poetry writing
3. only road traffic

Answer: 1
Explanation: Dams can support irrigation and generate electricity.

## 3. Concern

Dams can also cause:

1. displacement and environmental issues
2. no social impact
3. instant equality

Answer: 1
Explanation: Large projects can displace people and affect the environment.

## 4. Rainwater harvesting

Rainwater harvesting means:

1. collecting and storing rainwater
2. wasting rainwater
3. heating river water

Answer: 1
Explanation: Rainwater harvesting collects rain so it can be saved and used later.

## 5. Conservation

Water conservation is needed because:

1. freshwater is limited
2. oceans are directly drinkable everywhere
3. rainfall is always equal

Answer: 1
Explanation: Freshwater is limited, so we need to use it carefully.
