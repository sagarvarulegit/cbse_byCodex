# Madam Rides the Bus chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Valli

Valli is:

1. eight years old
2. eighteen years old
3. a bus conductor

Answer: 1
Explanation: Valli is a young girl of eight years.

## 2. Bus ride

Valli wants to ride the bus because she feels:

1. curious about the outside world
2. afraid of roads
3. tired of school tests only

Answer: 1
Explanation: Valli is curious and wants to see the world beyond her home.

## 3. Planning

Valli saves money by:

1. avoiding small temptations
2. borrowing from everyone
3. asking the conductor first

Answer: 1
Explanation: She carefully avoids spending on little things so she can save for the ride.

## 4. Conductor

The conductor calls her:

1. madam
2. auntie
3. princess

Answer: 1
Explanation: The conductor respectfully calls Valli madam.

## 5. Lesson

The dead cow scene teaches Valli about:

1. life and death
2. only bus tickets
3. cooking

Answer: 1
Explanation: The scene shows Valli a serious truth about life and death.
