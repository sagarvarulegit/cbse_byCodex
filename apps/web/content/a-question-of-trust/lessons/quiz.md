# A Question of Trust chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Horace

Horace Danby is outwardly:

1. respectable
2. careless and loud
3. a schoolboy

Answer: 1
Explanation: Horace looks honest and respectable to everyone, which is why he can hide his secret life so well.

## 2. Secret life

Secretly, Horace:

1. robs safes
2. teaches in school
3. writes poems only

Answer: 1
Explanation: He is a safecracker who breaks into houses secretly, even though he appears ordinary outside.

## 3. Woman

The young woman is actually:

1. another thief
2. the police officer
3. a neighbour only

Answer: 1
Explanation: She pretends to be a harmless visitor, but she is really a thief who tricks Horace.

## 4. Trust

Horace is trapped because he:

1. trusts the wrong person
2. forgets his shoes
3. closes the door too fast

Answer: 1
Explanation: He believes the woman's story, and that misplaced trust gives her the chance to outsmart him.

## 5. Irony

The story is ironic because:

1. a thief gets deceived by a thief
2. nobody enters the house
3. the safe is empty from the beginning

Answer: 1
Explanation: The twist is funny and ironic because Horace, who steals from others, is himself tricked by another thief.

## revision reminder

Recheck the hidden theft, the woman trick, and the twist ending before retrying.
