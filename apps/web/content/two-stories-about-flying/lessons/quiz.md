# Two Stories about Flying chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. First story

Topic: young-seagull-s-courage
Difficulty: Easy
Source: NCERT First Flight, Two Stories about Flying, His First Flight

The young seagull is afraid of:

1. flying
2. water only
3. trees only

Answer: 1
Explanation: The first story begins with the young seagull afraid to leave the ledge and fly.

## 2. Courage

Topic: young-seagull-s-courage
Difficulty: Easy
Source: NCERT First Flight, Two Stories about Flying, His First Flight

The seagull finally flies because of:

1. hunger
2. a train ticket
3. rain boots

Answer: 1
Explanation: Hunger pushes the young seagull toward food, and that desperate movement makes him discover he can fly.

## 3. Black aeroplane

Topic: the-mysterious-pilot-helper
Difficulty: Medium
Source: NCERT First Flight, Two Stories about Flying, The Black Aeroplane

The second story is mysterious because:

1. a black aeroplane appears and no one explains it
2. the pilot stays at home
3. the storm disappears instantly

Answer: 1
Explanation: The narrator is guided through the storm, but the control room later says no other aeroplane was seen.

## 4. Wild animals

Topic: wild-animals
Difficulty: Easy
Source: NCERT First Flight poem, How to Tell Wild Animals

The poem is:

1. humorous and exaggerated
2. purely scientific
3. only sad

Answer: 1
Explanation: The poem uses funny, exaggerated clues for identifying animals, so its tone is humorous rather than scientific.

## 5. Ball poem

Topic: ball-poem
Difficulty: Medium
Source: NCERT First Flight poem, The Ball Poem

The ball symbolises:

1. loss and growing up
2. a school prize only
3. a flying machine

Answer: 1
Explanation: The lost ball helps the child experience loss and begin understanding responsibility and growing up.
