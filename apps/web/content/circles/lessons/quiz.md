# Circles chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Radius

A radius is a line segment from:

1. center to the circle
2. one point on the circle to another
3. the circle to a tangent only

Answer: 1
Explanation: A radius joins the center of the circle to a point on the circle.

## 2. Chord

A chord is:

1. a line through the center only
2. a segment joining two points on the circle
3. a line touching the circle at one point

Answer: 2
Explanation: A chord joins any two points on the circle.

## 3. Diameter

The diameter is:

1. twice the radius
2. half the radius
3. equal to the circumference

Answer: 1
Explanation: The diameter is twice the radius of the circle.

## 4. Tangent

A tangent touches a circle at:

1. two points
2. one point
3. no points

Answer: 2
Explanation: A tangent touches a circle at exactly one point.

## 5. Secant

A secant is a line that:

1. does not meet the circle
2. meets the circle at two points
3. is always a radius

Answer: 2
Explanation: A secant intersects the circle at two points.
