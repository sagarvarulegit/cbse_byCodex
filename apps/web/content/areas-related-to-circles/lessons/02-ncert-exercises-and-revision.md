# NCERT Exercises and Chapter Revision

## Exercise 11.1

The fourteen questions cover:

- direct sector area and quadrant area
- clock-hand sweep area
- minor/major segment areas
- corresponding minor/major sector areas
- a horse grazing at the corner of a square field
- the circular brooch, umbrella and table-cover figures
- car-wiper swept area
- lighthouse light spread
- a multiple-choice sector formula

### Region method

1. Identify the full circle, sector, triangle and required shaded part.
2. Write the central angle.
3. Calculate each simple region separately.
4. Add or subtract only in the final region equation.

The active curriculum restricts segment calculations to central angles $60^\circ$, $90^\circ$ and $120^\circ$.

## Common mistakes

- treating the length of a clock hand or wiper as a diameter
- finding one blade's area when the question asks for two blades
- forgetting overlap or non-overlap information

## Board-answer checklist

- Required region named
- Correct radius and angle
- Area units in every final answer
- Approximation only after the exact expression

## One-minute revision

Make a small region equation before doing arithmetic: required area = added parts - removed parts.
