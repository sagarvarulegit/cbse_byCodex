# The Proposal chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Lomov

Lomov visits Chubukov to:

1. propose marriage
2. buy a horse
3. borrow a book

Answer: 1
Explanation: Lomov comes to propose to Natalya, even though the meeting quickly turns into an argument.

## 2. Natalya

Natalya is:

1. Chubukov's daughter
2. Lomov's teacher
3. the servant

Answer: 1
Explanation: Natalya is Chubukov's daughter and the person Lomov wants to marry.

## 3. First quarrel

The first quarrel is about:

1. Oxen Meadows
2. a necklace
3. a bus ride

Answer: 1
Explanation: Lomov and Natalya start arguing over the land called Oxen Meadows.

## 4. Second quarrel

The second quarrel is about:

1. dogs
2. crops
3. diaries

Answer: 1
Explanation: The second argument starts when they fight over whose dog is better.

## 5. Genre

The play is a:

1. farce
2. tragedy only
3. travel essay

Answer: 1
Explanation: The play is a comic farce because the quarrels are exaggerated and funny.

## revision reminder

Recheck Lomov's purpose, the two quarrels, and the comic style before retrying.
