# Outcomes of Democracy chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Accountability

A democratic government is accountable because:

1. citizens can question and replace it
2. citizens have no voice
3. elections never happen

Answer: 1
Explanation: Democracy is accountable because citizens can question and replace leaders.

## 2. Responsiveness

A responsive government:

1. considers people's needs
2. ignores all demands
3. bans criticism

Answer: 1
Explanation: A responsive government listens to people's needs and concerns.

## 3. Legitimacy

Democracy is legitimate because it is based on:

1. consent of citizens
2. force only
3. secrecy

Answer: 1
Explanation: Democratic legitimacy comes from the consent of citizens.

## 4. Economic growth

Democracy:

1. does not automatically guarantee fastest growth
2. always removes poverty instantly
3. stops development

Answer: 1
Explanation: Democracy supports growth, but it does not guarantee the fastest growth automatically.

## 5. Dignity

Democracy promotes dignity by:

1. recognising equal political status
2. removing rights
3. silencing citizens

Answer: 1
Explanation: Democracy gives citizens equal political dignity.
