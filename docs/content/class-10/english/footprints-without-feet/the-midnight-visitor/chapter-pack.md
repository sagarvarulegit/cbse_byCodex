# The Midnight Visitor

## chapter goal

By the end of this chapter, a student should be able to:
- explain how Ausable outwits Max
- describe Fowler's expectations and surprise
- understand suspense, wit, and presence of mind
- answer inference questions on spy fiction conventions

## teacher note on textbook alignment

This pack follows `jefp103.pdf`: Ausable's ordinary appearance, Fowler's disappointment, Max's threat, the invented balcony story, Henry's knock, and Max's fall.

## chapter flow for the platform

1. Fowler's expectation
2. Ausable's unusual spy image
3. Max enters with a pistol
4. The balcony trick
5. Henry's knock
6. Ausable's victory

## 1) Fowler's expectation

Fowler expects a glamorous secret agent surrounded by danger and mystery.

Ausable appears ordinary, fat, and unimpressive, so Fowler feels disappointed.

## 2) Max's threat

Max enters Ausable's room with a pistol and wants an important report.

The danger is real, but Ausable remains calm.

## 3) balcony trick

Ausable invents a story about a balcony outside his window.

There is no balcony, but Max believes him because Ausable speaks confidently.

## 4) Henry's knock

When Henry knocks at the door, Ausable says it is the police.

Max tries to escape through the imaginary balcony and falls.

## 5) message

The story shows that intelligence and presence of mind can defeat physical threat.

Ausable is a successful spy because he thinks quickly, not because he looks dramatic.

## exam-friendly summary notes

- Ausable does not look like a typical spy.
- Max threatens him for a secret report.
- Ausable invents the balcony.
- Max is fooled by calm confidence.
- The real midnight visitor is Henry, the waiter.

## common mistakes students make

1. Saying the balcony was real.
2. Forgetting Fowler's role as observer.
3. Thinking Ausable wins by force instead of wit.

## board tips

- In suspense answers, mention pistol, report, knock, and balcony.
- For Ausable's character, use calmness and intelligence.
- Explain how stereotypes about spies are reversed.

## short answer practice

1. Why was Fowler disappointed?
2. Why did Max come to Ausable's room?
3. How did Ausable create the balcony story?
4. Why did Max jump?

## mini quiz with answers

3. Max carries a pistol.
4. The balcony is imaginary.
5. Henry is the waiter.
