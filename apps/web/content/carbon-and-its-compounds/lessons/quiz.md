# Carbon and its Compounds chapter quiz

## 1. Carbon's bonding choice

Why does carbon generally form covalent bonds?

1. It has no valence electrons
2. Losing or gaining four electrons is energetically difficult
3. It can form only negative ions
4. Its outermost shell is already complete

Answer: 2
Topic: covalent-bonding-in-carbon
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 4.1 aligned
Explanation: Carbon has four valence electrons; complete transfer of four electrons is unfavourable, so it shares electrons.

## 2. Nitrogen molecule

How many shared electron pairs form the bond in `N2`?

1. one
2. two
3. three
4. four

Answer: 3
Topic: covalent-bonding-in-carbon
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Figure 4.4 aligned
Explanation: Each nitrogen needs three electrons, so the atoms share three pairs and form a triple bond.
