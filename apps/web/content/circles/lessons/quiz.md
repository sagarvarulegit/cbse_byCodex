# Circles chapter quiz

## 1. Tangent

A tangent to a circle meets the circle at:

1. no point
2. exactly one point
3. exactly two points
4. infinitely many points

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 10.2 aligned
Explanation: That single common point is called the point of contact.

## 2. Radius and tangent

At the point of contact, the radius is:

1. parallel to the tangent
2. perpendicular to the tangent
3. equal to the tangent
4. a secant

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Theorem 10.1 aligned
Explanation: The radius through the point of contact makes a right angle with the tangent.

## 3. Number of tangents

How many tangents can be drawn to a circle from a point outside it?

1. $0$
2. $1$
3. $2$
4. infinitely many

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 10.3 aligned
Explanation: Two tangent segments meet the circle at two different points of contact.

## 4. Equal tangents

From an external point $P$, tangents $PA$ and $PB$ touch a circle. Then:

1. $PA>PB$
2. $PA<PB$
3. $PA=PB$
4. $PA+PB=0$

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Theorem 10.2 aligned
Explanation: Tangent lengths drawn from the same external point are equal.

## 5. Tangent length

A circle has centre $O$ and radius $5$ cm. If $OP=13$ cm and $PT$ is tangent, then $PT$ is:

1. $8$ cm
2. $10$ cm
3. $12$ cm
4. $18$ cm

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Section 10.3 aligned
Explanation: $OT\perp PT$, so $PT=\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=12$ cm.

## 6. Interior point

The number of tangents from a point inside a circle is:

1. $0$
2. $1$
3. $2$
4. $4$

Answer: 1
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 10.3 aligned
Explanation: Any line through an interior point cuts the circle at two points, so it is not tangent.

## 7. Proof idea

The equal-tangents theorem is proved by showing two right triangles are congruent using:

1. AAA
2. RHS
3. SSS only
4. ASA only

Answer: 2
Topic: ncert-exercises-and-revision
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Theorem 10.2 aligned
Explanation: The radii are equal, $OP$ is common, and each radius is perpendicular to its tangent.

## 8. Circumscribed quadrilateral

If a quadrilateral touches a circle on all four sides, which relation follows from equal tangent pairs?

1. Sum of one pair of opposite sides equals the sum of the other pair
2. All four sides are always equal
3. Both diagonals are diameters
4. Every angle is $90^\circ$

Answer: 1
Topic: ncert-exercises-and-revision
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Exercise 10.2 aligned
Explanation: Equal tangent segments from each vertex cancel pairwise when opposite-side sums are compared.
