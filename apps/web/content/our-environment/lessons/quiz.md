# Mastery Quiz

1. Ecosystem equals? **Answer:** Interacting biotic community plus abiotic environment.
2. First trophic level? **Answer:** Producers.
3. Second level? **Answer:** Herbivores/primary consumers.
4. Arrow direction? **Answer:** Food to consumer/energy flow.
5. Food web? **Answer:** Interconnected food chains.
6. Average energy transfer? **Answer:** About 10%.
7. Plants capture about what solar fraction? **Answer:** About 1%.
8. Is energy recycled? **Answer:** No, it flows one way and dissipates as heat.
9. Decomposer examples? **Answer:** Bacteria and fungi.
10. Decomposer role? **Answer:** Break dead matter and recycle nutrients.
11. Biomagnification? **Answer:** Rising persistent-pollutant concentration up trophic levels.
12. Maximum pollutant level? **Answer:** Top consumer.
13. Ozone formula? **Answer:** O3.
14. Ozone shield blocks? **Answer:** Harmful UV radiation.
15. Main NCERT depletion chemicals? **Answer:** CFCs.
16. Biodegradable means? **Answer:** Decomposable by biological agents.
17. Can biodegradable waste pollute? **Answer:** Yes, if excessive/unmanaged.
18. Best first waste action? **Answer:** Refuse/reduce generation.
19. Wet organic treatment? **Answer:** Composting/biogas.
20. E-waste route? **Answer:** Authorised segregated collection/recycling.
