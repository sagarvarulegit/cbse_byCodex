## Draft boundary and follow-up

The five packs remain `status: draft`. The rebuild covers textbook order, source-labelled elements, exact end-exercise prompts, concise scoring answers and platform support sections. It does not claim final publication approval, legal updating beyond the supplied Reprint 2026–27 textbook, or browser-tested layout approval.

No lesson files or application routes were edited during the content-only rebuild. The final Social Science integration later regenerated the shared chapter-pack index and confirmed all 22 canonical Social Science keys. Tests, builds, lint, quiz validators and browser sweeps remained intentionally unrun under the repository’s current verification preference.
