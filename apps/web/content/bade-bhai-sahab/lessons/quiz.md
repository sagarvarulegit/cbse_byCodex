# Bade Bhai Sahab chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Relationship

The lesson focuses on:

1. two brothers
2. two kings
3. two shopkeepers

Answer: 1
Explanation: The lesson is about the relationship between the elder and younger brothers.

## 2. Elder brother

The elder brother values:

1. discipline and study
2. only games
3. careless habits

Answer: 1
Explanation: The elder brother keeps stressing study, rules, and discipline, even if he is a little comical about it.

## 3. Younger brother

The younger brother is more:

1. playful
2. strict
3. silent

Answer: 1
Explanation: The younger brother is still a child and likes play, freedom, and fun more than strict discipline.

## 4. Theme

The lesson compares:

1. experience and education
2. metals and non-metals
3. transport modes

Answer: 1
Explanation: The story shows the contrast between book study and practical life experience through the two brothers.

## 5. Tone

The story has:

1. humour and affection
2. only fear
3. no relationship

Answer: 1
Explanation: The tone is light and affectionate because the narrator treats the brotherly rivalry with warmth and humour.
