# Nationalism in India chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Satyagraha

Satyagraha means:

1. force of truth and non-violence
2. use of weapons first
3. rule by kings

Answer: 1
Explanation: Satyagraha is the force of truth and non-violence.

## 2. Non-Cooperation

The Non-Cooperation Movement asked people to:

1. boycott colonial institutions
2. join foreign armies only
3. stop all farming

Answer: 1
Explanation: The movement asked people to boycott colonial institutions.

## 3. Salt March

The Salt March was led by:

1. Mahatma Gandhi
2. Subhas Chandra Bose
3. Lord Curzon

Answer: 1
Explanation: Mahatma Gandhi led the Salt March.

## 4. Civil Disobedience

Civil Disobedience included:

1. breaking unjust colonial laws peacefully
2. supporting every law
3. leaving the country

Answer: 1
Explanation: Civil disobedience meant peacefully breaking unjust colonial laws.

## 5. Collective belonging

National symbols helped create:

1. a shared sense of belonging
2. only market prices
3. rainfall patterns

Answer: 1
Explanation: Symbols helped people feel part of one nation.
