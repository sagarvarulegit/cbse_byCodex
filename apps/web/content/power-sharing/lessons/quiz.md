# Power Sharing chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Meaning

Power sharing means:

1. distributing power among different groups or levels
2. keeping all power with one person
3. ending democracy

Answer: 1
Explanation: Power sharing means distributing power among groups and levels.

## 2. Belgium

Belgium used power sharing to manage:

1. linguistic and regional differences
2. crop seasons only
3. mineral exports

Answer: 1
Explanation: Belgium used power sharing to manage language and regional differences.

## 3. Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka followed:

1. majoritarianism
2. equal power sharing from the start
3. no language policy

Answer: 1
Explanation: Sri Lanka followed majoritarianism rather than equal sharing.

## 4. Democracy

Power sharing is desirable because it:

1. reduces conflict and supports democracy
2. increases dictatorship
3. removes all elections

Answer: 1
Explanation: Power sharing reduces conflict and strengthens democracy.

## 5. Forms

Power can be shared among:

1. organs of government, levels, groups, and parties
2. only one family
3. only one village

Answer: 1
Explanation: Power can be shared in several forms across government and groups.
