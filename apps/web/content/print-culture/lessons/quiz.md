# Print Culture and the Modern World chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Print culture

Print culture refers to:

1. spread and use of printed material
2. mining minerals
3. growing crops

Answer: 1
Explanation: Print culture is the spread and use of printed material.

## 2. Gutenberg

Gutenberg is associated with:

1. printing press in Europe
2. steam engine only
3. Salt March

Answer: 1
Explanation: Gutenberg is linked with the printing press in Europe.

## 3. Print revolution

Print helped ideas spread:

1. faster and wider
2. only inside palaces
3. without books

Answer: 1
Explanation: Printing let ideas spread faster and to more people.

## 4. India

Print in India helped:

1. public debate and reform
2. stop all reading
3. close all schools

Answer: 1
Explanation: Print encouraged public debate and reform in India.

## 5. Censorship

Censorship means:

1. control over what can be printed or circulated
2. a farming method
3. a tax on rainfall

Answer: 1
Explanation: Censorship is control over publishing and circulation.
