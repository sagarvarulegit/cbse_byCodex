# Class 10 Civics source-fidelity tracker

This tracker records the source-review scope for the five Reprint 2026–27 Political Science/Civics chapter packs. It is an editorial audit aid, not student-facing chapter content.

## Review method and status

- Source PDFs were reviewed only from the repository paths listed in frontmatter.
- All **74 of 74 PDF pages** were rendered as full-page images and visually inspected at original detail. Text extraction was used only to lock exact headings, captions, data and exercise wording.
- Every pack follows the printed textbook heading order and introduces no invented topic numbering.
- Every end exercise is present with its printed wording and a concise answer.
- Every mapped figure, photograph, map, cartoon, table, activity, data feature, story and glossary/special box is listed and explained inside the relevant textbook section.
- All five packs remain **draft**. Visual source inspection is complete, but platform editorial review and final publication approval have not been performed.
- During the source-only rebuild, no generated output, tests, builds, validators or browser sweeps were run. The final Social Science integration later regenerated the shared chapter-pack index and confirmed all 22 canonical Social Science keys.

## Source anomalies and editorial decisions

- In `jess404.pdf`, the National parties section says there are **six recognised national parties** under the 2023 Election Commission notification and supplies six profiles. The following State parties paragraph nevertheless begins “Other than these seven parties”. The pack uses the explicit count and six profiles, and does not silently convert the contradiction into a seventh party.
- The INC profile in `jess404.pdf` prints “Leader of the United Progressive Alliance (UPA) government from 2004 to 2019.” This date range is source wording. Any future factual correction must be handled as an explicit textbook erratum, not an untracked rewrite.
- Data, maps and recognition lists retain their printed dates. The 2018 regional-party map and historical survey/table figures are explained as dated evidence, not current live data.
- Exercise wording retains source spellings and phrasing where meaningful. Answers use accessible language but do not change the tested concept.
