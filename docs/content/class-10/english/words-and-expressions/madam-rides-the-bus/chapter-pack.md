# Words and Expressions Unit 7 - Madam Rides the Bus

## chapter goal

By the end of this workbook unit, a student should be able to:
- discuss Valli's journey and changing feelings
- describe personal travel experiences
- read travel/adventure passages for detail and inference
- practise vocabulary, grammar, editing, speaking, writing, and project work

## teacher note on textbook alignment

This pack follows `jewe207.pdf`: discussion on Valli's bus ride, personal journey sharing, reading comprehension on travel adventure, vocabulary, grammar, editing, listening/speaking, writing, and project work.

## 1) Valli discussion

The unit asks what Valli saw from the bus and whether her excitement stayed the same on return.

A strong answer mentions the joyful outward journey and the serious mood after seeing death on the return.

## 2) personal travel

Students share journeys by bus, train, bicycle, boat, or other means.

Good speaking includes route, purpose, sights, feelings, and one memorable incident.

## 3) travel reading

Travel passages should be read for place, people, movement, challenge, and emotional response.

Look for words that show courage, adventure, age, health, and discovery.

## 4) vocabulary

Useful vocabulary includes journey, route, destination, landscape, strenuous, altitude, adventure, and experience.

## 5) writing

Travel writing should move in order: before journey, during journey, important moment, and reflection.

Use sensory details and clear sequence.

## exam-friendly summary notes

- Valli's return journey is emotionally different.
- Travel writing needs sequence and observation.
- Personal experience should include feelings and details.
- Reading passages may combine adventure and reflection.
- Vocabulary should match movement and place.

## common mistakes students make

1. Describing only scenery and not feelings.
2. Ignoring the change in Valli's mood.
3. Writing travel experiences without sequence.

## board tips

- Use chronological order in journey writing.
- Include one turning point or memorable moment.
- In discussion, compare outward and return journeys.

## short answer practice

1. What excited Valli during the bus ride?
2. Why was the return journey different?
3. What details make travel writing effective?
4. How should you organise a travel paragraph?
