# A Letter to God chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Lencho

Lencho is a:

1. farmer
2. shopkeeper
3. teacher

Answer: 1
Explanation: Lencho is the farmer whose crops are destroyed by hail, so the story starts with him as a farmer.

## 2. Faith

Lencho has:

1. little faith
2. complete faith in God
3. no hope at all

Answer: 2
Explanation: Lencho is sure that God will help him, so his faith is complete and very strong.

## 3. Hailstorm

The crop is destroyed by:

1. rain only
2. hailstorm
3. drought

Answer: 2
Explanation: The storm drops hailstones that ruin the crop, not normal rain or drought.

## 4. Postmaster

The postmaster helps Lencho because he feels:

1. amused only
2. moved by faith and kindness
3. angry at the letter

Answer: 2
Explanation: The postmaster is touched by Lencho's faith, so he and the staff help him out of kindness.

## 5. Irony

The ending is ironic because:

1. Lencho thinks the money came from God, not from the helpers
2. God visits the valley
3. there is no letter

Answer: 1
Explanation: The irony is that Lencho thanks God for the help, while the money actually comes from the postmaster and his staff.

Recheck faith, irony, and the postmaster's kindness before retrying.
