# Class 10 Mathematics source-fidelity tracker

This tracker maps the NCERT Mathematics textbook, Reprint 2026-27, to the platform's lesson-wise content. The local PDFs above are the content authority. The active CBSE Mathematics Standard (041) curriculum decides board scope.

## Scope boundary

The following older topics may remain as clearly labelled support, but they are not core 2026-27 board coverage:

- Euclid division lemma and decimal expansions in Chapter 1
- division algorithm for polynomials in Chapter 2
- cross-multiplication in Chapter 3
- completing the square in Chapter 4
- coordinate-triangle area in Chapter 7
- frustum of a cone in Chapter 12
- ogive construction in Chapter 13

The current NCERT chapter PDFs already omit most of these topics. Platform notes must not restore them as numbered core sections.
