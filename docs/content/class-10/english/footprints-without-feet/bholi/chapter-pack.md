# Bholi

## chapter goal

By the end of this chapter, a student should be able to:
- describe Bholi's childhood and transformation
- explain the teacher's role in building confidence
- analyse the rejection of Bishamber's dowry demand
- answer questions on education, dignity, and social prejudice

## 1) Bholi's childhood

Bholi, whose real name is Sulekha, is neglected because of her appearance, slow development, and stammer.

Others mock her, so she becomes quiet and fearful.

## 2) family attitude

Her family does not value her education at first.

She is sent to school mainly because the family thinks she has poor marriage prospects.

## 3) teacher's role

Bholi's teacher treats her with kindness and respect.

She encourages Bholi to speak and learn. This gives Bholi confidence and self-respect.

## 4) marriage proposal

Bishamber agrees to marry Bholi but demands dowry after seeing her face.

This exposes greed and social cruelty.

## 5) Bholi's courage

Bholi refuses to marry him. She speaks clearly and stands up for her dignity.

Her education has transformed her from a frightened child into a self-respecting woman.

## exam-friendly summary notes

- Bholi is neglected and mocked as a child.
- Her teacher changes her life through kindness.
- Education gives her confidence.
- Bishamber's dowry demand reveals greed.
- Bholi chooses dignity over marriage.

## common mistakes students make

1. Writing that Bholi changes suddenly without education.
2. Ignoring the teacher's emotional support.
3. Treating marriage as the solution to Bholi's life.

## board tips

- For transformation, write before school and after education.
- Mention dowry as a social evil.
- Use dignity and self-respect in value answers.
