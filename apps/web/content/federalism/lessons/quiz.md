# Federalism chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Meaning

Federalism means:

1. power divided between central and state governments
2. power held by one king only
3. no government

Answer: 1
Explanation: Federalism divides power between different levels of government.

## 2. Constitution

In federalism, power division is usually:

1. constitutionally defined
2. hidden from people
3. decided every morning

Answer: 1
Explanation: The constitution clearly defines how power is shared.

## 3. India

India has:

1. Union, State, and Concurrent Lists
2. only one local list
3. no states

Answer: 1
Explanation: India's Constitution uses the Union, State, and Concurrent Lists.

## 4. Decentralisation

Decentralisation means:

1. sharing power with local governments
2. removing local bodies
3. ending elections

Answer: 1
Explanation: Decentralisation pushes power closer to local governments and communities.

## 5. Panchayati Raj

Panchayati Raj is linked with:

1. local self-government
2. global trade only
3. mineral extraction

Answer: 1
Explanation: Panchayati Raj is India's local self-government system.
