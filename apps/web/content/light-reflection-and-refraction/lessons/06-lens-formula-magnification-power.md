# 9.3.6 Lens Sign Convention

Use optical centre O as origin and incident light left to right.

- real object: `u < 0`;
- convex lens: `f > 0`; concave lens: `f < 0`;
- real image to right: `v > 0`, inverted `h' < 0`;
- virtual image to left: `v < 0`, erect `h' > 0`.

# 9.3.7 Formulae

`1/f = 1/v - 1/u`

`m = h'/h = v/u`

Notice: lens formula uses a **minus** before `1/u`, and lens magnification has
no extra minus sign.

## Worked convex-lens numerical

`h=5 cm, u=-25 cm, f=+10 cm`

`1/v + 1/25 = 1/10`, so `v=+16.7 cm`.

`m=v/u=-0.667`; `h'=-3.33 cm`.

Image: 16.7 cm on the other side, real, inverted, diminished.

## Worked concave-lens numerical

`f=-15 cm, v=-10 cm`

`-1/10 - 1/u = -1/15`, so `u=-30 cm`.

The image is virtual, erect, and diminished.

# 9.3.8 Power of a Lens

`P = 1/f(in metre)`

Unit: dioptre `D`.

- convex/converging lens: positive power;
- concave/diverging lens: negative power.

For thin lenses in contact: `Ptotal = P1 + P2 + ...`.

Examples:

- `P=-2.0 D -> f=-0.50 m`: concave lens.
- `P=+1.5 D -> f=+0.667 m`: convex lens.

## Formula-selection card

| Device | Formula | Magnification |
|---|---|---|
| mirror | `1/f=1/v+1/u` | `m=-v/u` |
| lens | `1/f=1/v-1/u` | `m=v/u` |
