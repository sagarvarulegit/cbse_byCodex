# Control and Coordination chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Nervous system

The nervous system helps in:

1. control and response
2. making food
3. dissolving salts

Answer: 1
Explanation: The nervous system controls body actions and helps us respond quickly.

## 2. Reflex

A reflex action is:

1. a slow planned action
2. a quick automatic response
3. a type of digestion

Answer: 2
Explanation: A reflex is a fast automatic response to a stimulus.

## 3. Brain

The brain is:

1. the control centre of the body
2. a blood cell
3. a bone joint

Answer: 1
Explanation: The brain coordinates and controls many body functions.

## 4. Hormone

Hormones are:

1. chemical messengers
2. body wastes
3. digestive enzymes only

Answer: 1
Explanation: Hormones are chemical messengers that travel through the body.

## 5. Plant movement

Plants coordinate by using:

1. only nerves
2. hormones
3. lungs

Answer: 2
Explanation: Plants respond and coordinate mainly through hormones, not nerves.
