# Educational video review rubric

Inspect the actual video, rendered frames, captions, and source material. Do not review from a title, thumbnail, code, or script alone when the video is available.

## Evidence sampling

- Inspect the opening, each causal transition, the formal explanation, the recap, and the final retrieval moment.
- For animation, review representative early/middle/late frames plus every frame where a label, equation, figure number, or state changes.
- Compare narration, captions, and visuals at the same timestamp.
- Record observed evidence separately from interpretation.
- Use the source-fidelity and accuracy ledgers from [production-templates.md](production-templates.md).

## Gates

### 1. Source fidelity

Pass only when:

- The scope and source are explicit.
- Numbering, terms, quantities, equations, figures, activities, and safety statements match the source.
- Platform additions are not presented as textbook content.
- No necessary mechanism has been removed.

### 2. Causal clarity

Pass only when the learner can state:

```text
What changed?
What caused it?
What evidence showed the change?
```

Flag unexplained jumps, magical transformations, or a label appearing before its meaning is visible.

### 3. Mental-model integrity

Pass only when visual mappings stay consistent and the analogy boundary is clear. Flag colour, motion, scale, or spatial changes that imply a false relationship.

### 4. Cognitive load

Pass only when each beat has one main learning job, text remains readable, and labels arrive when needed. Flag dense simultaneous narration, captions, motion, and labels.

### 5. Story usefulness

Pass only when curiosity, characters, conflict, failed attempts, humour, and suspense advance understanding. Cut entertainment beats that delay or distort the mechanism.

### 6. Retrieval and retention

Pass only when the ending asks the learner to retrieve or apply the idea. A repeated slogan without a prediction, explanation, label, comparison, or worked step is not active recall.

### 7. Accessibility and safety

Pass only when:

- Narration has synchronized captions.
- Meaning does not depend on colour alone.
- Labels, formulas, and figure numbers are readable on the target screen.
- Flashing, motion, and sound do not create avoidable barriers.
- Safety guidance appears before risky actions.

### 8. Production coherence

Pass only when timing, narration, motion, audio, and scene transitions are intentional and the output is integrated into the requested destination.

## Review output

Lead with the verdict and the most important learning effect. Then report:

```markdown
## What works
- <observed technique -> learner benefit>

## What blocks understanding
- <timestamp/beat -> problem -> likely misconception>

## Accuracy risks
- <claim/visual -> source conflict or uncertainty -> correction>

## Highest-value revisions
1. <specific revision>
2. <specific revision>
3. <specific revision>

## Verification
- Inspected:
- Source checked:
- Intentionally skipped:
```

Prioritize no more than three revisions unless the user requests a comprehensive audit. Fix source or causal errors before pacing, polish, music, or decorative motion.
