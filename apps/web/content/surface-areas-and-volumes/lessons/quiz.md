# Surface Areas and Volumes chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Cube

The volume of a cube with side `a` is:

1. `6a^2`
2. `a^3`
3. `a^2`

Answer: 2
Explanation: The volume of a cube is `a^3`.

## 2. Cuboid

The volume of a cuboid is:

1. `lbh`
2. `2(l+b+h)`
3. `l+b+h`

Answer: 1
Explanation: The volume of a cuboid is length times breadth times height.

## 3. Sphere

The surface area of a sphere is:

1. `4pi r^2`
2. `2pi r`
3. `pi r^3`

Answer: 1
Explanation: The surface area of a sphere is `4pi r^2`.

## 4. Cylinder

The volume of a cylinder is:

1. `pi r^2 h`
2. `2pi r h`
3. `4pi r^2 h`

Answer: 1
Explanation: The volume of a cylinder is `pi r^2 h`.

## 5. Unit idea

Volume is measured in:

1. square units
2. cubic units
3. line units

Answer: 2
Explanation: Volume is measured in cubic units.
