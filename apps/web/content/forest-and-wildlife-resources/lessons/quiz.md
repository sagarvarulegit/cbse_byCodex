# Forest and Wildlife Resources chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Biodiversity

Biodiversity means:

1. variety of living organisms
2. only one crop type
3. number of factories

Answer: 1
Explanation: Biodiversity refers to the variety of living things.

## 2. Flora

Flora means:

1. plant life
2. animal life
3. mineral ores

Answer: 1
Explanation: Flora means the plant life of a region.

## 3. Fauna

Fauna means:

1. animal life
2. soil type only
3. rainfall pattern

Answer: 1
Explanation: Fauna means the animal life of a region.

## 4. Project Tiger

Project Tiger protects:

1. tigers and their habitats
2. only roads
3. only rivers

Answer: 1
Explanation: Project Tiger aims to protect tigers and their living spaces.

## 5. Conservation

Conservation works best when:

1. communities and government both participate
2. forests are ignored
3. wildlife is hunted freely

Answer: 1
Explanation: Conservation works best when local people and government work together.
