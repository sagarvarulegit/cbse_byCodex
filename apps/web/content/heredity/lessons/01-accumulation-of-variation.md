# 8.1 Accumulation of Variation During Reproduction

Reproduction produces offspring that share the parents' basic body design, but
DNA copying is not perfectly accurate. Small differences therefore arise.

<ActivityIllustration demoId="figure-8-1-variation-generations" />

## How variation accumulates

Suppose one bacterium produces two slightly different offspring. Each offspring
reproduces and passes on its inherited differences, while new variations may
also arise. Thus later generations contain:

`inherited variations + newly created variations`

- Asexual reproduction produces relatively little variation because one parent
  supplies the DNA.
- Sexual reproduction produces more variation because two parents contribute
  different DNA combinations.
- The number of successful variations is limited by the accuracy of DNA copying
  and by whether a variation permits the organism to survive.

## Why variation matters

Variation does not guarantee an advantage to an individual. However, when the
environment changes, a useful variant may survive and reproduce while others do
not. Variation therefore improves the survival chances of a **species**.

Example: in a warming water body, heat-resistant bacteria may survive and
multiply even if most bacteria die.

## NCERT questions

### If trait A occurs in 10% and trait B in 60% of an asexually reproducing population, which arose earlier?

Trait B is likely to have arisen earlier because it has had more generations in
which to be inherited and accumulate, assuming neither trait gives a special
survival advantage.

### How does variation promote survival?

Different variants respond differently to an environmental change. A variant
suited to the new condition may survive and reproduce, preventing the entire
species from being eliminated.

## Board-ready distinction

| Point | Individual | Species |
|---|---|---|
| Effect of a variation | May be useful, neutral, or harmful | Diversity increases the chance that some members survive change |
| Correct conclusion | Every variation is **not** beneficial | Variation is beneficial to long-term species survival |

## Remember

`variation in one generation -> inheritance + new variation -> accumulation`

Do not write that organisms produce useful variations because they “need” them.
Variations arise first; the environment selects among them.
