# Arithmetic Progressions chapter quiz

## 1. Recognising an AP

Which list is an arithmetic progression?

1. $1,2,4,8,\ldots$
2. $3,7,11,15,\ldots$
3. $1,4,9,16,\ldots$
4. $2,3,5,8,\ldots$

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 5.2 aligned
Explanation: Consecutive terms differ by the constant value $4$.

## 2. Common difference

The common difference of $18,15,12,9,\ldots$ is:

1. $3$
2. $-3$
3. $-6$
4. $15$

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 5.2 aligned
Explanation: $d=a_2-a_1=15-18=-3$.

## 3. The nth term

The $20$th term of the AP $5,9,13,\ldots$ is:

1. $76$
2. $77$
3. $81$
4. $85$

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 5.3 aligned
Explanation: $a_{20}=5+19(4)=81$.

## 4. Testing a term

Is $301$ a term of the AP $5,11,17,\ldots$?

1. Yes, it is the $49$th term
2. Yes, it is the $50$th term
3. No, because the term number is not a whole number
4. No, because $301$ is odd

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Exercise 5.2 aligned
Explanation: $301=5+(n-1)6$ gives $n=50\tfrac{1}{3}$, not a natural-number position.

## 5. Sum formula

Which formula gives the sum of the first $n$ terms of an AP?

1. $S_n=n(a+d)$
2. $S_n=\frac n2[2a+(n-1)d]$
3. $S_n=a+(n-1)d$
4. $S_n=\frac n2(a-d)$

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 5.4 aligned
Explanation: The bracket contains twice the first term plus the total common-difference increase.

## 6. Sum of natural numbers

The sum $1+2+3+\cdots+20$ is:

1. $190$
2. $200$
3. $210$
4. $220$

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 5.4 aligned
Explanation: $S_{20}=20(1+20)/2=210$.

## 7. Counting terms in context

How many two-digit numbers are divisible by $3$?

1. $29$
2. $30$
3. $31$
4. $33$

Answer: 2
Topic: ncert-exercises-and-revision
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Exercise 5.2 aligned
Explanation: The AP is $12,15,\ldots,99$; $99=12+(n-1)3$ gives $n=30$.
