# From the Diary of Anne Frank chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Diary

Anne names her diary:

1. Kitty
2. Molly
3. Daisy

Answer: 1
Explanation: Anne Frank names her diary Kitty.

## 2. Writing style

Diary writing is:

1. personal and reflective
2. only formal and public
3. always rhyming

Answer: 1
Explanation: Diary writing is personal and reflective because it records private thoughts.

## 3. Teacher

Mr Keesing is Anne's:

1. principal
2. teacher
3. friend from home

Answer: 2
Explanation: Mr Keesing is Anne's teacher.

## 4. Punishment

Anne gets essays because she:

1. is talkative
2. is absent every day
3. refuses all homework

Answer: 1
Explanation: Mr Keesing gives Anne essays because she talks too much in class.

## 5. Theme

The diary shows Anne's need for:

1. a true friend to confide in
2. a cricket bat
3. a bicycle only

Answer: 1
Explanation: Anne wants a true friend so she can share her thoughts and feelings.
