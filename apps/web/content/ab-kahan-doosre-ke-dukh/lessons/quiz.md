# Ab Kahan Doosre Ke Dukh Se Dukhi Hone Wale chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Main concern

The chapter is about loss of:

1. empathy toward others and nature
2. electricity formula
3. election symbols

Answer: 1
Explanation: The poem warns that people lose empathy when they ignore the suffering of others and the natural world.

## 2. Modern life

The chapter criticises:

1. concrete urban life without sensitivity
2. caring for animals
3. forest conservation

Answer: 1
Explanation: The poet criticises modern life when it becomes cold, mechanical, and insensitive.

## 3. Nature

The lesson asks humans to:

1. live with compassion for nature
2. ignore all living beings
3. cut every tree

Answer: 1
Explanation: The poem asks people to respect nature and live with compassion instead of harming it.

## 4. Human value

The central value is:

1. empathy
2. greed
3. pride only

Answer: 1
Explanation: Empathy is the main human value the poet wants readers to protect.

## 5. Message

The lesson reminds us that:

1. others' pain should matter
2. only our comfort matters
3. nature has no value

Answer: 1
Explanation: The message is that the pain of others matters and should shape how we live.

## revision reminder

Recheck empathy, nature, and the warning against insensitive modern life.
