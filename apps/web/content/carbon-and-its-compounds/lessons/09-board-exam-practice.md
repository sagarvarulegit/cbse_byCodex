# Board Exam Practice

These questions represent every distinct Chapter 4 demand family found in the
local 2023-2026 CBSE paper archive, together with the latest official CBSE

## PYQ 1 - 2023, 31/4/1, Q34, 5 marks

### Option A

1. Draw butanoic acid and chloropentane.
2. Explain how the two displayed `C6H14` structures are related and draw one
   more possible structure.
3. Differentiate saturated and unsaturated compounds using general formulae.

### OR Option B

1. What happens when sodium is added to ethanol? Write the equation.
2. Why is pure acetic acid called glacial acetic acid?
3. Heat ethanol with concentrated `H2SO4` at 443 K: product, equation and role
   of the acid.
4. Write a saponification equation.

### Platform marking points

**A:** `CH3CH2CH2COOH`; one valid chloropentane structure; the shown
structures are isomers because the molecular formula is the same but carbon
skeleton differs; one further valid isomer is n-hexane,
`CH3-CH2-CH2-CH2-CH2-CH3`; open-chain alkane
`CnH2n+2`, alkene `CnH2n`, alkyne `CnH2n-2`.

**B:** Hydrogen and sodium ethoxide form:
`2Na + 2C2H5OH → 2C2H5ONa + H2`. Pure ethanoic acid freezes near 290 K and
forms ice-like crystals. Dehydration:
`C2H5OH --conc. H2SO4, 443 K--> C2H4 + H2O`; the acid is a dehydrating agent.
Saponification:
`CH3COOC2H5 + NaOH → CH3COONa + C2H5OH`.

## PYQ 2 - 2023, 31/4/3, Q36, 5 marks

Write balanced equations for combustion of methane, oxidation of ethanol,
hydrogenation of ethene, esterification and saponification.

### Platform marking points

1. `CH4 + 2O2 → CO2 + 2H2O + heat`
2. `CH3CH2OH + 2[O] → CH3COOH + H2O`
3. `CH2=CH2 + H2 --Ni--> CH3CH3`
4. `CH3COOH + C2H5OH ⇌ CH3COOC2H5 + H2O`
5. `CH3COOC2H5 + NaOH → CH3COONa + C2H5OH`

Write the condition/catalyst above the arrow; it is a separate marking point.

## PYQ 3 - 2024, 31/1/1, Q34, 5 marks

### Option A

Define a homologous series; explain the higher boiling/melting points of a
four-carbon member than smaller members; explain why chemical properties do
not show such gradation; draw and name the `C3H6O` aldehyde and ketone.

### OR Option B

Identify the two-carbon alcohol `X`; heat it with excess concentrated
`H2SO4` at 443 K; give product, equation, acid's role, and the product's
electron-dot structure.

### Platform marking points

**A:** Same functional group/general formula, `CH2` and 14 u difference.
Larger molecular mass strengthens intermolecular attraction, raising physical
constants. Chemical properties stay similar because the functional group is
unchanged. `CH3CH2CHO` is propanal; `CH3COCH3` is propanone.

**B:** `X` is ethanol, `C2H5OH`. It loses water to form ethene:
`C2H5OH --conc. H2SO4, 443 K--> CH2=CH2 + H2O`. Concentrated sulphuric acid
acts as dehydrating agent. In ethene, the carbon atoms share two pairs with
each other and one pair with each of two hydrogen atoms.

## PYQ 4 - 2024, 31/1/2, Q34, 5 marks

### Option A

Define functional group; identify the groups in the displayed structures;
write ethanol oxidation with acidified potassium dichromate and explain why it
is oxidation; write ethanoic acid plus sodium hydroxide.

### OR Option B

Describe soap preparation with equation and explain cleansing action using a
diagram.

### Platform marking points

**A:** A functional group is an atom/group that gives characteristic chemical
properties. Identify `-OH`, `-CHO`, `>C=O` or `-COOH` from their bonding, not
from oxygen alone. Ethanol gains oxygen:
`CH3CH2OH + 2[O] → CH3COOH + H2O`.
Neutralisation:
`CH3COOH + NaOH → CH3COONa + H2O`.

**B:** Heat a fat/oil with sodium hydroxide:
`fat/oil + NaOH → soap + glycerol`. Salt separates the soap. In the diagram,
tails enter grease and ionic heads face water; agitation lifts the micelle and
rinsing removes it.

## PYQ 5 - 2024, 31/4/1, Q22, 2 marks

Distinguish saturated and unsaturated hydrocarbons by flame test. List the
products of complete combustion of a saturated hydrocarbon.

### Platform marking points

A saturated hydrocarbon usually burns with a clean blue flame, whereas an
unsaturated hydrocarbon generally burns with a yellow sooty flame. Complete
combustion produces `CO2`, `H2O`, heat and light. Limited oxygen can make even
a saturated hydrocarbon burn sootily, so state the usual equal-air comparison.

## PYQ 6 - 2024, 31/4/1, Q28, 3 marks

Why is ethanol-to-ethanoic-acid conversion oxidation? Name the oxidising agent,
write the equation, and distinguish it from burning ethanol in oxygen.

### Platform marking points

Ethanol gains oxygen:
`CH3CH2OH + 2[O] → CH3COOH + H2O`.
Use alkaline `KMnO4` or acidified `K2Cr2O7`. Controlled oxidation keeps the
two-carbon chain and gives ethanoic acid; complete combustion breaks it down
to `CO2 + H2O` with heat and light.

## PYQ 7 - 2024, 31/4/2, Q22, 2 marks

Add dilute ethanoic acid to sodium carbonate. Write the equation and test the
gas.

### Platform marking points

`2CH3COOH + Na2CO3 → 2CH3COONa + H2O + CO2`

Pass the gas through lime water. It turns milky because calcium carbonate
forms:
`Ca(OH)2 + CO2 → CaCO3 + H2O`.

## PYQ 8 - 2025 compartment, 31/S/1, Q37, 4 marks

1. Name `CH3-C≡CH`.
2. Compare saturated and unsaturated flames.
3. Show an addition reaction of a suitable hydrocarbon.

### OR

Define structural isomers and draw two isomers of butane.

### Platform marking points

`CH3-C≡CH` is **propyne**. Use the clean-blue versus yellow-sooty comparison.
Addition example:
`CH2=CH2 + H2 --Ni--> CH3CH3`.
OR: same molecular formula, different structures; draw n-butane and
2-methylpropane.

## PYQ 9 - 2026, 31/4/1, Q20, 1 mark

In a soap micelle, which orientation is correct?

### Platform marking points

Hydrophobic tails lie in the interior in grease; hydrophilic ionic heads face
outward toward water. This is **option A**.

## PYQ 10 - 2026, 31/4/1, Q24, Assertion-Reason

Assertion: Pure ethanoic acid is called glacial acetic acid.  
Reason: It often freezes in winter because its melting point is 290 K.

### Platform marking points

Both statements are true and the Reason explains the Assertion: ice-like
crystals form near/below 290 K. Choose **A**.

## PYQ 11 - 2026, 31/5/1, Q28

1. Give the fourth alcohol homolog, its structure and name.
2. Oxidise ethanol with alkaline `KMnO4`.
3. Write esterification with ethanoic acid.

### OR for part 3

Dehydrate ethanol at 443 K and state the role of concentrated `H2SO4`.

### Platform marking points

The fourth member is butanol, `CH3CH2CH2CH2OH`.
Oxidation:
`CH3CH2OH + 2[O] → CH3COOH + H2O`.
Esterification:
`CH3COOH + C2H5OH ⇌ CH3COOC2H5 + H2O`.
OR dehydration:
`C2H5OH --conc. H2SO4, 443 K--> C2H4 + H2O`; the acid removes water.

## PYQ 12 - 2026 visually-impaired paper, Q29, 5 marks

### Option A

Define catenation and explain carbon's catenation; define homologous series
with two members differing by 14 u; explain why soap fails in hard water.

### OR Option B

Explain low melting/boiling points of covalent compounds; name an ethanol
oxidising agent; explain why ethanoic acid is weaker than mineral acids;
define esterification; define micelles.

### Platform marking points

Every answer is taught directly in Lessons 1-7. The last-mile keywords are:
strong `C-C` bonds; same functional group and `CH2`; calcium/magnesium scum;
weak intermolecular forces; alkaline `KMnO4`/acidified `K2Cr2O7`; partial
ionisation of ethanoic acid; acid + alcohol gives ester + water; and tails-in,
heads-out clusters.

# Latest official CBSE SQP - 2025-26, Q29, 5 marks

`2CxHy + 9O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O`

Find `x` and `y`, name the hydrocarbon, draw its electron-dot structure, name
the alcohol that dehydrates to it, and hydrogenate it with nickel.

### Official marking points

- Carbon balance: `2x = 6`, so `x = 3`.
- Hydrogen balance: `2y = 12`, so `y = 6`.
- Hydrocarbon: **propene**, `C3H6`; it is unsaturated.
- Electron-dot/line structure: `CH2=CH-CH3`.
- Alcohol: propanol.
- Hydrogenation:
  `CH2=CH-CH3 + H2 --Ni--> CH3-CH2-CH3`.

# Last-mile scoring checklist

- Attempt one complete OR option; do not mix halves.
- For structures, check carbon valency four and keep the requested molecular
  formula unchanged.
- For equations, balance atoms and add catalyst/temperature/light.
- For “distinguish”, state both observations using the same test.
- For an activity, write apparatus/reagent, observation, inference and safety.
- For a micelle diagram, tails point inward and hydrophilic heads face water.
- For a reason, name the controlling idea: functional group, intermolecular
  force, bond type, ion formation or solubility.
