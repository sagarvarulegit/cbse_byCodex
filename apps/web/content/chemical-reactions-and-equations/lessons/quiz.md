# Chemical Reactions and Equations chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Reaction

A chemical reaction is:

1. a change that produces new substances
2. only a physical shape change
3. only a temperature reading

## 2. Equation balancing

Chemical equations are balanced to follow:

1. law of conservation of mass
2. law of motion
3. law of reflection

## 3. Combination

In a combination reaction:

1. one product forms from two or more reactants
2. one reactant always breaks apart into two products
3. nothing changes at all

## 4. Oxidation

Oxidation often means:

1. gain of oxygen or loss of hydrogen
2. loss of oxygen only
3. no exchange at all

## 5. Displacement

In a displacement reaction:

1. a more reactive element replaces a less reactive one
2. all reactants disappear
3. only gases are produced
