# Manufacturing Industries chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Manufacturing

Manufacturing means:

1. processing raw materials into valuable goods
2. only selling vegetables
3. only counting population

Answer: 1
Explanation: Manufacturing changes raw materials into goods with higher value.

## 2. Location

Industrial location depends on:

1. raw material, power, labour, market, and transport
2. only poetry
3. only rainfall names

Answer: 1
Explanation: Industries usually choose locations with access to inputs, labour, markets, and transport.

## 3. Agro-based

Cotton textiles are:

1. agro-based industry
2. mineral-based only
3. marine industry only

Answer: 1
Explanation: Cotton textiles depend on agricultural raw material, so they are agro-based.

## 4. Basic industry

Iron and steel is called a basic industry because:

1. many industries depend on it
2. it grows in fields
3. it is never used

Answer: 1
Explanation: Many other industries rely on iron and steel as a base.

## 5. Pollution

Industrial pollution can be reduced by:

1. treating waste and using cleaner methods
2. releasing untreated waste
3. ignoring laws

Answer: 1
Explanation: Cleaner production and waste treatment reduce industrial pollution.
