# Arithmetic Progressions chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Common difference

In an arithmetic progression, the difference between consecutive terms is:

1. always changing
2. constant
3. always zero

## 2. nth term

The nth term formula of an AP is:

1. `a + (n - 1)d`
2. `a^2 + d`
3. `an + d^2`

## 3. First term

In the AP `3, 7, 11, 15, ...`, the first term is:

1. `3`
2. `7`
3. `11`

## 4. Sum formula

The sum of first `n` terms of an AP is:

1. `n/2 [2a + (n - 1)d]`
2. `a + d`
3. `an + d`

## 5. Pattern idea

An AP is best described as a sequence with:

1. a constant difference
2. a constant ratio
3. no pattern at all

## revision reminder

Focus on first term, common difference, nth term, and sum formula together.
