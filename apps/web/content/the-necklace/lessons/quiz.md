# The Necklace chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Matilda

Matilda is unhappy because she wants:

1. luxury and beauty
2. hard labour only
3. no friends

Answer: 1
Explanation: Matilda keeps dreaming about wealth, elegance, and a richer life, so she feels unhappy with her plain reality.

## 2. Necklace

She loses a:

1. borrowed diamond necklace
2. school bag
3. watch

Answer: 1
Explanation: She borrows a diamond necklace for the party and then loses it, which starts the central problem.

## 3. Replacement

The replacement causes:

1. ten years of debt
2. a single day of rest
3. no change at all

Answer: 1
Explanation: Buying a replacement necklace forces Mathilde and Loisel into years of hardship and debt.

## 4. Theme

The story warns against:

1. vanity and false pride
2. learning from friends
3. simple living

Answer: 1
Explanation: The story shows how vanity and pride can lead to serious loss and suffering.

## 5. Ending

The ending is:

1. ironic
2. magical
3. musical

Answer: 1
Explanation: The ending is ironic because the truth about the necklace comes too late and completely changes the meaning of their sacrifice.

Recheck vanity, loss, replacement, and the twist ending before retrying.
