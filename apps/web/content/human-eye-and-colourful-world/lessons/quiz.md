# Human Eye and Colourful World chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Retina

The retina is the part of the eye where:

1. image is formed
2. sound is produced
3. blood is filtered

Answer: 1
Explanation: The retina is the light-sensitive screen where the image is formed.

## 2. Myopia

Myopia is corrected using:

1. concave lens
2. convex mirror only
3. glass slab only

Answer: 1
Explanation: A concave lens helps correct myopia.

## 3. Hypermetropia

Hypermetropia is corrected using:

1. convex lens
2. concave lens only
3. prism only

Answer: 1
Explanation: A convex lens helps correct hypermetropia.

## 4. Dispersion

Dispersion splits:

1. white light into colours
2. current into charge
3. water into soil

Answer: 1
Explanation: Dispersion separates white light into its constituent colours.

## 5. Sky colour

The sky appears blue mainly due to:

1. scattering of light
2. magnetic effect
3. evaporation

Answer: 1
Explanation: Blue sky is mainly due to scattering of sunlight.
