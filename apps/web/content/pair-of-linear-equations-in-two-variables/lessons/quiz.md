# Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables chapter quiz

## 1. Intersecting lines

Two lines intersect once when:

1. $a_1/a_2=b_1/b_2=c_1/c_2$
2. $a_1/a_2=b_1/b_2\ne c_1/c_2$
3. $a_1/a_2\ne b_1/b_2$
4. every coefficient is zero

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Table 3.1 aligned
Explanation: Different coefficient ratios for x and y give different slopes and one intersection.

## 2. Coincident lines

Coincident lines have:

1. no solution
2. exactly one solution
3. infinitely many solutions
4. two solutions

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 3.2 aligned
Explanation: Every point on one line also lies on the other.

## 3. Parallel pair

The pair $x+2y=3$ and $2x+4y=9$ is:

1. intersecting
2. coincident
3. parallel and inconsistent
4. not linear

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Exercise 3.1 aligned
Explanation: $1/2=2/4\ne3/9$.

## 4. Direct solution

Solve $x+y=5$ and $x-y=1$.

1. $(2,3)$
2. $(3,2)$
3. $(4,1)$
4. $(1,4)$

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT algebraic methods aligned
Explanation: Adding gives $2x=6$, so $x=3$ and $y=2$.

## 5. Meaning of $0=0$

If substitution reduces the second equation to $0=0$, the pair generally has:

1. no solution
2. one solution
3. infinitely many solutions
4. non-real solutions

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Example 6 aligned
Explanation: One equation is dependent on the other.

## 6. Meaning of a false statement

If elimination produces $0=9$, the pair has:

1. no solution
2. one solution
3. infinitely many solutions
4. $x=0,y=9$

Answer: 1
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Example 9 aligned
Explanation: A false equality shows the two conditions cannot hold together.

## 7. Digit problem

A two-digit number has digit sum $6$, and its digits differ by $2$. Which pair of numbers can satisfy the conditions?

1. $15$ and $51$
2. $24$ and $42$
3. $33$ only
4. $26$ and $62$

Answer: 2
Topic: ncert-exercises-and-revision
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Example 10 aligned
Explanation: Digits $4$ and $2$ have sum $6$ and difference $2$.

## 8. Word-problem finish

After solving a cost problem, what should the final line contain?

1. only the ordered pair

4. the graph scale
