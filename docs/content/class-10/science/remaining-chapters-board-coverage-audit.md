# Science Chapters 2-13 Board-Coverage Audit

## Scope and method

- Textbook baseline: complete current NCERT Science PDFs `jesc102.pdf` through
  `jesc113.pdf`, reviewed page by page.
- Board baseline: local CBSE Class 10 Science archive containing 165 PDFs from
  2022-2026 and 3,266 extracted question instances.
- Official-answer baseline: available CBSE marking schemes for 2023-2026.
- Audit unit: a distinct demand family, not a raw keyword hit. Generic OCR hits,
  adjacent-chapter questions, duplicates, and alternate sets were classified
  before judging coverage.
- Passing rule: the platform must contain the fact/concept, the required
  reasoning chain or derivation, a board-length answer method, and numerical or
  diagram practice wherever the archive demands it.

## Answerability gate

The classified archive produced no retained demand family for Chapters 2-13
whose required knowledge or solving method is absent from the published lesson
packs. Each chapter includes:

1. NCERT-numbered concept lessons in source order;
2. activity observations and figure-based reasoning;
3. complete NCERT in-text and end-exercise answers;
4. a dedicated board-exam-practice lesson;
5. a 20-question mastery quiz;
6. a chapter-level source-fidelity tracker and board-evidence record.

This is a coverage conclusion, not a guarantee that marks can be earned without
practice, recall, clear presentation, and correct execution under exam
conditions. The platform now supplies the content and answer methods needed to
respond to the archived demand families.
