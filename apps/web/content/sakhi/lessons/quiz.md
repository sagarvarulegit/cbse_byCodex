# Sakhi chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Poet

The sakhis are by:

1. Kabir
2. Meera
3. Premchand

Answer: 1
Explanation: The sakhis are by Kabir.

## 2. Speech

Kabir values:

1. sweet and humble speech
2. harsh words
3. silence without thought

Answer: 1
Explanation: Kabir values humble and kind speech.

## 3. God

Kabir says God is:

1. within us
2. only in wealth
3. nowhere

Answer: 1
Explanation: Kabir teaches that God can be found within us.

## 4. Ego

The sakhis ask us to reduce:

1. ego
2. kindness
3. truth

Answer: 1
Explanation: The sakhis warn against ego and pride.

## 5. Message

The poems teach:

1. inner devotion and humility
2. greed and pride
3. anger as strength

Answer: 1
Explanation: The poems teach devotion, humility, and inner reflection.

Recheck Kabir's message about speech, ego, and inner devotion.
