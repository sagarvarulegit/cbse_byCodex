# Life Processes chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Nutrition

Nutrition is the process of:

1. getting and using food
2. making rocks
3. changing light into sound

Answer: 1
Explanation: Nutrition is how living things obtain and use food.

## 2. Respiration

Respiration releases:

1. stored energy
2. only water
3. only oxygen

Answer: 1
Explanation: Respiration releases stored energy from food.

## 3. Transport

In humans, blood transports:

1. only waste
2. oxygen, nutrients, and waste materials
3. only minerals from soil

Answer: 2
Explanation: Blood carries oxygen, nutrients, and waste materials around the body.

## 4. Excretion

Excretion means:

1. removal of metabolic waste
2. digestion of food
3. intake of oxygen

Answer: 1
Explanation: Excretion is the removal of metabolic waste from the body.

## 5. Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis happens mainly in:

1. leaves
2. bones
3. the stomach

Answer: 1
Explanation: Photosynthesis mainly happens in leaves where chlorophyll is present.
