# Madam Rides the Bus

## chapter goal

By the end of this unit, a student should be able to:
- describe Valli's desire, planning, and bus journey
- explain the shift from excitement to maturity
- identify the theme of childhood curiosity and first encounter with death
- revise "The Tale of Custard the Dragon"
- answer questions on character, irony, and humour

## teacher note on textbook alignment

This pack follows `jeff107.pdf`: Valli's bus journey, her observations, the dead cow episode, and the poem "The Tale of Custard the Dragon".

## chapter flow for the platform

1. Valli's curiosity
2. Planning the journey
3. The bus ride to town
4. Return journey and dead cow
5. Growth in understanding
6. Poem: "The Tale of Custard the Dragon"
7. Exam practice and recap

## 1) Valli's curiosity

Valli is an eight-year-old girl who loves watching the street from her doorway.

The bus fascinates her because it connects her small village to the larger outside world.

## 2) planning the journey

Valli carefully gathers information about the bus fare, timing, and route.

She saves money by resisting small temptations. This shows determination and self-control.

## 3) bus ride

On the bus, Valli behaves with pride and independence.

She notices people, scenery, shops, and the lively world outside her village.

The conductor treats her with humour and calls her "madam".

## 4) return journey

On the return journey, Valli sees the same cow that had earlier been playful now lying dead.

This shocks her and changes her mood. She begins to understand that joy and death can exist in the same world.

## 5) growth

The journey becomes more than an adventure. It becomes Valli's first serious lesson about life and death.

She does not explain everything to her family because the experience is private and deep.

## 6) poem: The Tale of Custard the Dragon

This humorous ballad presents Custard as a supposedly cowardly dragon.

When danger arrives, Custard proves to be the bravest and saves everyone.

Theme: true courage is shown in action, not in boasting.

## exam-friendly summary notes

- Valli is curious, observant, bold, and self-controlled.
- Her bus ride symbolises entry into the wider world.
- The dead cow teaches her about death.
- The conductor adds humour.
- Custard shows real courage despite being mocked.

## common mistakes students make

1. Calling Valli careless; she plans carefully.
2. Ignoring the emotional impact of the dead cow.
3. Treating the poem as only a funny animal story.
4. Missing the contrast between boasting and bravery.

## board tips

- For Valli, write curiosity + planning + maturity.
- In the poem, use Custard to explain true courage.
- Mention tone change in the story: excitement to seriousness.

## short answer practice

1. Why did Valli want to ride the bus?
2. How did she prepare for the journey?
3. Why did the dead cow affect her?
4. Why is Custard called brave?
5. What is the difference between boasting and courage?
