# NCERT Exercises and Chapter Revision

## Exercise 12.1

The nine surface-area questions use combinations of cubes, cuboids, cylinders, cones, spheres and hemispheres. Figures 12.10-12.11 show the capsule and scooped cylinder.

For each object:

1. split it into basic solids
2. mark joined faces as hidden
3. mark depressions as exposed inner surfaces
4. add only the surfaces visible in the final object

The tent question explicitly says that its base is not covered with canvas.

## Exercise 12.2

The eight volume questions cover:

- a cone on a hemisphere
- a hollow rocket model
- gulab jamuns and syrup volume in Figure 12.15
- a cuboid pen stand with conical depressions in Figure 12.16
- a vessel with a rising water level
- a cone-cylinder-sphere iron pole
- a cone standing on a hemisphere and water displacement
- a spherical glass vessel compared with its cylindrical-looking capacity

### Displacement rule

Volume of solid placed in water = volume of water displaced, provided the solid is completely submerged.

## Common mistakes

- using outer dimensions for a hollow object's inner volume
- forgetting the number of identical depressions or objects
- comparing capacities before converting all measurements to the same unit

## Board-answer checklist

- Component solids named
- Join/removal operation justified
- Common unit used
- Square or cubic unit correct

## One-minute revision

Build one clean expression from the geometry before reaching for the calculator.

## Comic recap

- Composite solid: "Break me into familiar pieces."
- Surface: "Count only what remains exposed."
- Volume: "Add material and subtract cavities."
