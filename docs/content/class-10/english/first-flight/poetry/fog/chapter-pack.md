# Fog

## chapter goal

Understand how the poem compares fog to a cat and creates a quiet visual image.

## teacher note on textbook alignment

This pack supports the poem paired with "Mijbil the Otter" in `jeff106.pdf`.

## chapter flow for the platform

1. Main image.
2. Cat metaphor.
3. Silence and movement.
4. Brevity.
5. Exam answer points.

## 1) main image

The poem presents fog as something that arrives quietly, stays for a while, and moves on.

The whole poem depends on one clear comparison.

## 2) cat metaphor

The cat image suggests soft movement, silence, patience, and sudden departure.

This makes the fog easy to see in the mind.

## 3) brevity

The poem is very short. Every word matters.

The quiet style matches the quiet movement of fog.

## exam-friendly summary notes

- Fog is compared to a cat.
- The metaphor shows silence and softness.
- The poem is brief and visual.
- The tone is calm and observant.

## common mistakes students make

1. Writing that the poem is actually about a pet.
2. Missing the metaphor.
3. Over-explaining a very short poem.

## board tips

- Use metaphor in answers.
- Mention silence and soft movement.
- Keep answers short and focused.

## short answer practice

1. What is fog compared to?
2. Why is the cat image suitable?
3. How does brevity help the poem?
