# 3.5-3.6 Occurrence, corrosion, and prevention

Corrosion is the slow damage of metals due to reaction with air, moisture, or chemicals.

## Learning goals

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:
- define corrosion
- explain conditions needed for rusting
- describe Activity 3.14
- list ways to prevent corrosion

## What is corrosion

Corrosion means slow eating away or damage of a metal surface.

Rusting is corrosion of iron.

Rust is hydrated iron(III) oxide.

## Conditions for rusting

Iron rusts when both oxygen and water are present.

If either air or moisture is missing, rusting is prevented or greatly reduced.

## Activity 3.14: Conditions for rusting

Three tubes compare normal water and air, boiled water with oil, and dry air with anhydrous calcium chloride. Only the tube with both air and water rusts strongly.

## Prevention methods

Rusting and corrosion can be reduced by keeping air and water away from the metal.

Common methods:
- painting
- oiling or greasing
- galvanisation
- electroplating
- alloying

Galvanisation means coating iron with zinc.

## Questions and answers

### 3.5-3.6 Occurrence, corrosion, and prevention

Question:
What are the conditions for rusting?

Answer:
Iron rusts when both oxygen and water are present.

Question:
How can rusting be prevented?

Answer:
Rusting can be prevented by painting, oiling, galvanising, electroplating, or alloying.

Question:
Why is galvanisation effective?

Answer:
Galvanisation coats iron with zinc, so air and water cannot easily reach the iron surface.

Think it over:
- Even a thin protective coating can stop rust from starting on iron.

## Solved examples

### Example 1: Rusting condition

Question:
Why does an iron nail rust in moist air?

Answer:
Moist air provides both oxygen and water, which are needed for rusting.

### Example 2: Galvanisation

Question:
How does galvanisation prevent rusting?

Answer:
Zinc coating protects iron from contact with air and moisture.

## Common mistakes

1. Saying rusting needs only oxygen.
2. Saying rusting needs only water.
3. Confusing galvanisation with painting.
4. Writing that corrosion happens only to iron.

## Board tips

- For rusting conditions, always write both oxygen and water.
- For prevention, explain how the method blocks contact with air or moisture.
- Remember corrosion is the general term; rusting is specific to iron.

## One-minute revision

Rusting needs both air and moisture. Prevention methods work by blocking air/water contact or using a protective coating.
