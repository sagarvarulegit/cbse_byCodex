## Accuracy decisions

- Ray diagrams and image tables cover every NCERT object position.
- Mirror and lens sign conventions are kept separate.
- Formulae use `1/f = 1/v + 1/u` for mirrors and `1/f = 1/v - 1/u`
  for lenses.
- A half-covered lens forms a complete but dimmer image.
- Figures 9.7, 9.8, and 9.12-9.17 use original NCERT-aligned vector redraws;
  labels are also provided in accessible HTML keys.
