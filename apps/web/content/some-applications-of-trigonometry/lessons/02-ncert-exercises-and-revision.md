# NCERT Exercises and Chapter Revision

## Exercise 9.1

The fifteen textbook questions cover:

- circus rope and broken-tree problems
- slide, kite and tower heights
- observer height and two-building models
- lighthouse, pole, canal and cable-tower situations
- balloon movement and an approaching car seen from a lighthouse

### Diagram checklist

- Ground, wall or water surface drawn horizontal
- Tower, pole, building or height drawn vertical
- Eye level drawn separately when given
- Elevation/depression angle marked at the correct horizontal
- Every given length copied with units

### Calculation checklist

- Use one right triangle at a time.
- In two-position problems, name the common height before forming equations.
- In a broken-tree problem, the broken part becomes the hypotenuse.
- In a shadow problem, the ground distance is the shadow length.
- In a balloon or car-motion problem, subtract the two ground distances after finding each one.

## Common mistakes

- adding two horizontal distances when the geometry requires subtraction
- treating a sloping cable or rope as a vertical height
- rounding $\sqrt3$ too early and carrying error through two triangles

## Board-answer checklist

- Neat labelled figure
- Correct trigonometric equation for each triangle
- Exact simplification
- Final measurement with unit and interpretation

## One-minute revision

Every word problem becomes manageable after the correct right triangles are separated.

## Comic recap

- Story: "Turn my words into a labelled sketch."
- Angle: "Place me at the horizontal."
- Triangle: "Solve one of us at a time."
- Measurement: "Report the exact value and units."
