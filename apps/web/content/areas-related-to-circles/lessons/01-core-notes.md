# 11.1-11.2 Areas Related to Circles

A pizza slice, a clock-hand sweep and a shaded chord region all use the same idea: take the required fraction of the full circle, then add or subtract the matching triangle when needed.

## 11.1 Areas of Sector and Segment of a Circle

Figures 11.1-11.2 distinguish minor/major sectors and minor/major segments.

For a circle of radius $r$ and central angle $\theta$ in degrees:

$$\text{arc length}=\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\times2\pi r,$$

$$\text{sector area}=\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\times\pi r^2.$$

Figures 11.3-11.4 show the matching sector and arc.

### Segment area

For a minor segment cut off by chord $AB$:

$$\text{segment area}=\text{sector area}-\text{area of }\triangle OAB.$$

For a major segment:

$$\text{major segment}=\pi r^2-\text{minor segment}.$$

**Example 1**

Figure 11.5 has $r=4$ cm and $\theta=30^\circ$.

$$\text{minor sector}=\frac{30}{360}\pi(4)^2\approx4.19\text{ cm}^2.$$

The corresponding major sector is about $46.1\text{ cm}^2$.

**Example 2**

Figures 11.6-11.7 use $r=21$ cm and $\theta=120^\circ$. The required segment is found by subtracting the isosceles triangle from the sector.

## 11.2 Summary

- arc length is the angle fraction of $2\pi r$
- sector area is the angle fraction of $\pi r^2$
- segment area equals corresponding sector minus triangle

## Common mistakes

- using diameter in place of radius
- subtracting the triangle when the question asks for a sector
- using the minor angle for a major sector without subtracting from $360^\circ$
- mixing square units and linear units

## Board tips

- Shade or name the required region before choosing operations.
- Keep the value of $\pi$ specified in the question.
- For $60^\circ$, $90^\circ$ and $120^\circ$ segments, identify the triangle type before finding its area.

## Quick practice

1. Find the area of a $60^\circ$ sector of radius $6$ cm.
2. Find the arc length of a $90^\circ$ sector of radius $14$ cm using $\pi=22/7$.
3. What must be subtracted to turn a minor sector into its minor segment?

## Answer check

1. $6\pi\text{ cm}^2$.
2. $22$ cm.
3. The area of the triangle formed by the two radii and chord.

## One-minute revision

Angle fraction first. Then decide whether the region is a sector or a segment.
