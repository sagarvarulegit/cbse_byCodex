# 2.3-2.4 pH scale and importance in daily life

pH is a simple scale that tells us how acidic or basic a solution is. The idea behind it is ion concentration in water.

## Learning goals

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:
- read the pH scale
- connect acids with `H+` ions
- connect bases with `OH-` ions
- explain why acids conduct electricity in water
- describe pH in everyday examples

## The pH scale

pH values usually run from 0 to 14.

- pH below 7 means acidic
- pH 7 means neutral
- pH above 7 means basic

Lower pH means stronger acidity. Higher pH means stronger basic nature.

Examples:
- lemon juice is acidic
- pure water is neutral
- soap solution is basic

## Ions in acids and bases

Acids produce hydrogen ions in water:

`HCl(aq) -> H+(aq) + Cl-(aq)`

Bases produce hydroxide ions in water:

`NaOH(aq) -> Na+(aq) + OH-(aq)`

The `(aq)` state matters because these ions move freely in water.

## Why dry acids do not show the same behaviour

Acids show acidic behaviour mainly in water because water helps release ions. Dry HCl gas does not turn dry blue litmus red, but moist HCl can.

The important idea is:

`acid + water -> ions that show acidic properties`

## Dilution and safety

When acid is mixed with water, heat is released. Always add acid to water slowly, not water to acid.

Memory line:

Add acid to water. Never add water to acid.

## Everyday pH

pH matters in daily life:
- tooth decay starts when mouth pH becomes too acidic
- antacids neutralise excess stomach acid
- soil pH affects plant growth
- bee sting and wasp sting relief depends on acid-base nature

## Questions and answers

### 2.3-2.4 pH scale and importance in daily life

Question:
What is the pH scale?

Answer:
The pH scale shows how acidic or basic a solution is. Values below 7 are acidic, 7 is neutral, and above 7 are basic.

Question:
Why do dry acids not show the same behaviour as acids in water?

Answer:
Acidic behaviour depends on ion formation, and ions form properly only in water.

Question:
How is the concentration of hydronium ions affected when an acid is diluted?

Answer:
Dilution lowers the concentration of hydronium ions, so the solution becomes less acidic.

More to Know:
- The pH scale is logarithmic, so a change of one unit means a tenfold change in acidity or basicity.

## Solved examples

### Example 1: Identify the solution

A solution has pH 3.

Answer:
It is acidic because its pH is below 7.

### Example 2: Explain conductivity

Dilute HCl conducts electricity.

Answer:
It produces mobile `H+` and `Cl-` ions in water.

## Common mistakes

1. Thinking pH 14 is strongest acid.
2. Forgetting that pH 7 is neutral.
3. Saying acids conduct electricity because they are hot.
4. Ignoring water in the formation of ions.

## Board tips

- In pH answers, compare with 7.
- Use `H+` for acids and `OH-` for bases.
- For conductivity, write "mobile ions" clearly.
- Mention safety when writing about dilution.
