# NCERT Exercises and Chapter Revision

## Exercise 2.1

Figure 2.10 asks for the number of zeroes of six displayed polynomial graphs.

**Answer:** Count the $x$-axis meeting points. The six answers are $0$, $1$, $3$, $2$, $4$ and $3$.

## Exercise 2.2

### 1. Find the zeroes and verify the relationship with the coefficients

| Polynomial | Zeroes | Sum | Product |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $x^2-2x-8$ | $4,-2$ | $2$ | $-8$ |
| $4s^2-4s+1$ | $1/2,1/2$ | $1$ | $1/4$ |
| $6x^2-7x-3$ | $3/2,-1/3$ | $7/6$ | $-1/2$ |
| $4u^2+8u$ | $0,-2$ | $-2$ | $0$ |
| $t^2-15$ | $\sqrt{15},-\sqrt{15}$ | $0$ | $-15$ |
| $3x^2-x-4$ | $4/3,-1$ | $1/3$ | $-4/3$ |

### 2. Find a quadratic polynomial from its given sum and product

One convenient answer for each pair is:

| Sum | Product | Polynomial |
| ---: | ---: | --- |
| $1/4$ | $-1$ | $4x^2-x-4$ |
| $\sqrt2$ | $1/3$ | $3x^2-3\sqrt2x+1$ |
| $0$ | $\sqrt5$ | $x^2+\sqrt5$ |
| $1$ | $1$ | $x^2-x+1$ |
| $-1/4$ | $1/4$ | $16x^2+4x+4$ |
| $4$ | $1$ | $x^2-4x+1$ |

**Remember:** Any non-zero multiple of a listed polynomial is also correct.

## Common mistakes

- reading the number of turning points instead of the number of zeroes
- losing a negative sign in $-b/a$
- treating $x^2-15$ as if its zeroes were $15$ and $-15$

## Board-answer checklist

- Factor or solve the polynomial.
- Write both zeroes.
- Verify the sum and product on separate lines.

## One-minute revision

Graph: count $x$-axis contacts. Algebra: compare the sum and product with $-b/a$ and $c/a$.
