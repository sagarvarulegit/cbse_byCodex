# NCERT Exercises and Chapter Revision

## Exercise 7.1

The eight printed questions cover:

- distances between numeric and symbolic point pairs
- the distance formula's opening town situation
- collinearity of three points
- checking an isosceles triangle
- the classroom grid in Figure 7.8
- identifying a quadrilateral from side and diagonal lengths
- equidistance from two fixed points
- finding a missing coordinate from a given distance

For a shape question, calculate every required side and diagonal. Equal sides alone may not identify the exact quadrilateral.

## Exercise 7.2

The ten questions cover:

- internal division in given ratios
- two trisection points
- the Sports Day flags in Figure 7.12
- missing ratios and coordinates
- a parallelogram's fourth vertex
- a point on the $x$-axis equidistant from two points
- points dividing a segment into four equal parts
- a ratio condition from two internal points
- a rhombus area extension marked productive

Useful checks:

- Trisection requires two separate ratios, $1:2$ and $2:1$.
- A point on the $x$-axis has $y=0$; a point on the $y$-axis has $x=0$.
- In a parallelogram, the diagonals have the same midpoint.

## Common mistakes

- using midpoint when the ratio is not $1:1$
- forgetting that a coordinate-axis condition fixes one coordinate
- taking a negative square-root value for distance

## Board-answer checklist

- Formula copied correctly
- Coordinates substituted in matching positions
- Exact value simplified before any decimal approximation
- Shape or location conclusion stated

## One-minute revision

Draw a tiny labelled segment before a section-formula question; it prevents most ratio reversals.

## Comic recap

- Grid: "Label both axes before reading me."
- Formula: "Keep each $x$ with an $x$ and each $y$ with a $y$."
- Ratio: "Say who divides whom."
- Conclusion: "Name the point, distance or shape."
