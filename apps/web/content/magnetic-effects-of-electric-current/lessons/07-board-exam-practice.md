# Board Exam Practice

## Recurring demand families

1. Draw bar-magnet field with correct arrows and properties.
2. Explain compass deflection near magnet/current.
3. Prove why field lines cannot intersect.
4. Draw straight-wire concentric field pattern.
5. Apply right-hand thumb rule to dot/cross or compass direction.
6. Explain field dependence on current and distance.
7. Draw circular-loop field and state strengthening factors.
8. Identify N/S face from clockwise/anticlockwise current.
9. Define solenoid and compare with bar magnet.
10. Explain uniform internal field and electromagnet strengthening.
11. Describe Activity 12.7 observations.
12. Apply Fleming left-hand rule to field/current/force cases.
13. State conditions for maximum conductor force.
14. Distinguish all three direction rules.
15. Explain 220 V, 50 Hz, live/neutral/earth and NCERT colours.
16. Explain domestic parallel connection and circuit ratings.
17. Explain earth wire and metal-body safety.
18. Distinguish overload from short circuit.
19. Explain fuse location and Joule-heating action.
20. Calculate appliance current and select safe circuit/fuse rating.

## Direction-question method

Draw three axes; convert electron direction to conventional current if needed;
mark field N→S; use the named rule; check that B, I, and F are mutually
perpendicular. State direction in words.

## Domestic-circuit five-mark frame

Supply rating -> live/neutral/earth -> meter/main protection -> parallel branches
-> independent switches -> earthing -> overload/short-circuit protection.

## High-value traps

- Field lines outside N→S, inside S→N.
- Dot is out of page; cross is into page.
- A solenoid field is not zero inside.
- Magnetic force can change direction without changing speed.
- Fuse belongs in live wire; earth is not the normal return path.
- Overload is excessive load current; short circuit is direct live-neutral
  contact.
