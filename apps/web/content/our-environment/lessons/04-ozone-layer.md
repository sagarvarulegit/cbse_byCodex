# 13.2 How Do Our Activities Affect the Environment?

Human activities change ecosystems; this chapter focuses on ozone depletion
and waste disposal.

# 13.2.1 Ozone Layer and How It Is Getting Depleted

Ozone `O3` contains three oxygen atoms. Near living organisms it is poisonous,
but high in the atmosphere it forms a shield absorbing harmful solar UV
radiation.

<ActivityIllustration demoId="figure-13-ozone-waste" />

## Formation

`O2 --UV--> O + O`

`O + O2 -> O3`

## Importance

UV can damage living tissues and cause effects such as skin cancer. Ozone-layer
damage therefore threatens humans and whole ecosystems.

## Depletion and action

CFCs used historically as refrigerants and in fire extinguishers were linked
to sharp ozone decline. NCERT records the 1987 UNEP agreement to freeze CFC
production at 1986 levels and the move to CFC-free refrigerators.

Board answer:

`CFC release -> ozone destruction -> more UV reaches Earth -> tissue/DNA damage`

Controls include international restriction/phasing out of ozone-depleting
substances, CFC-free technology, leak prevention, and safe refrigerant
recovery.

Activity 13.4 asks students to research responsible chemicals and whether
regulations have reduced ozone-layer damage.

## Common trap

The greenhouse effect and ozone depletion are different problems. Do not answer
“global warming” when asked how ozone protects life; name UV radiation.
