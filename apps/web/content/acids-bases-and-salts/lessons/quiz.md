# Acids, Bases and Salts chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Acid

An acid usually tastes:

1. sweet
2. sour
3. salty

Answer: 2
Explanation: Acids are usually sour, so option 2 matches the basic property.

## 2. Base

A base usually feels:

1. slippery
2. crunchy
3. metallic

Answer: 1
Explanation: Bases often feel slippery or soapy to the touch.

## 3. pH

A pH below 7 usually shows:

1. a basic solution
2. an acidic solution
3. a neutral solution

Answer: 2
Explanation: A pH below 7 means the solution is acidic.

## 4. Indicator

Litmus is used to:

1. identify acids and bases
2. measure mass
3. make salts directly

Answer: 1
Explanation: Litmus is an indicator that helps tell acids from bases.

## 5. Neutralisation

Neutralisation is the reaction between:

1. acid and base
2. salt and water
3. oxygen and nitrogen

Answer: 1
Explanation: Neutralisation happens when an acid reacts with a base.
