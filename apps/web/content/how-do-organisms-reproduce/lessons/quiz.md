# How do Organisms Reproduce? chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Asexual reproduction

Asexual reproduction involves:

1. one parent
2. exactly two parents always
3. no living cells

Answer: 1
Explanation: Asexual reproduction happens from a single parent.

## 2. Sexual reproduction

Sexual reproduction usually involves:

1. one parent only
2. fusion of male and female gametes
3. no gametes at all

Answer: 2
Explanation: Sexual reproduction involves the fusion of male and female gametes.

## 3. Binary fission

Binary fission is common in:

1. bacteria and Amoeba
2. only humans
3. only flowering plants

Answer: 1
Explanation: Binary fission is a common asexual method in bacteria and Amoeba.

## 4. Pollination

Pollination is the transfer of:

1. pollen grains
2. blood cells
3. oxygen molecules

Answer: 1
Explanation: Pollination transfers pollen grains from one flower part to another.

## 5. Reproductive health

Reproductive health includes:

1. care, hygiene, and awareness
2. only height growth
3. only protein digestion

Answer: 1
Explanation: Reproductive health includes hygiene, awareness, and proper care.
