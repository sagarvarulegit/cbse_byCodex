# Nelson Mandela: Long Walk to Freedom chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Apartheid

Apartheid was:

1. a racial segregation system
2. a dance style
3. a trade route

Answer: 1
Explanation: Apartheid was the system of racial segregation in South Africa.

## 2. Inauguration

The inauguration marks:

1. a democratic beginning
2. the end of all speeches
3. a school assembly

Answer: 1
Explanation: The inauguration marks the beginning of a democratic government.

## 3. Courage

Mandela says courage is:

1. overcoming fear
2. never feeling fear
3. winning every game

Answer: 1
Explanation: Courage means overcoming fear, not pretending fear does not exist.

## 4. Obligations

Mandela talks about duty to:

1. family and community
2. books and chairs
3. weather and roads

Answer: 1
Explanation: Mandela speaks about responsibility to family, community, and society.

## 5. Freedom

Freedom, in Mandela's view, includes:

1. responsibility
2. selfishness
3. silence only

Answer: 1
Explanation: Mandela links freedom with responsibility, not selfishness.

## revision reminder

Recheck apartheid, courage, and freedom with responsibility before retrying.
