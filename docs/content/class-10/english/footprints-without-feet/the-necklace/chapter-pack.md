# The Necklace

## chapter goal

By the end of this chapter, a student should be able to:
- explain Matilda's dissatisfaction and pride
- retell the necklace incident and twist ending
- understand irony and the danger of vanity
- answer value-based questions on honesty and appearance

## teacher note on textbook alignment

This pack follows `jefp107.pdf`: Matilda's dreams of luxury, the invitation, borrowed necklace, loss, years of hardship, and the revelation that the necklace was imitation.

## chapter flow for the platform

1. Matilda's dissatisfaction
2. Invitation and borrowed necklace
3. Party and loss
4. Years of repayment
5. Twist ending
6. Theme and irony

## 1) Matilda

Matilda is pretty but unhappy because she dreams of wealth and social status.

She feels ashamed of her simple life and compares herself with richer people.

## 2) invitation

Her husband brings an invitation to a grand party, hoping to make her happy.

She wants a fine dress and jewellery because appearance matters deeply to her.

## 3) necklace

Matilda borrows a necklace from her friend Madame Forestier.

She looks beautiful at the party but loses the necklace afterward.

## 4) hardship

Matilda and her husband replace the necklace with an expensive one and spend years repaying debt.

Their life becomes harsh because they choose not to tell the truth.

## 5) twist ending

At the end, Matilda learns that the original necklace was imitation and worth much less.

This creates deep irony: years of suffering were caused by vanity, fear, and silence.

## exam-friendly summary notes

- Matilda is dissatisfied with her simple life.
- She borrows and loses a necklace.
- The Loisels replace it with an expensive necklace.
- They suffer for years to repay debt.
- The original necklace was fake.
- The story warns against vanity and dishonesty.

## common mistakes students make

1. Saying Matilda was poor; she was modestly placed but dissatisfied.
2. Forgetting her husband's sacrifice.
3. Missing the irony of the fake necklace.

## board tips

- In theme answers, mention vanity, appearance, honesty, and fate.
- Explain irony clearly.
- Use Matilda's change from beauty to hardship.

## short answer practice

1. Why was Matilda unhappy?
2. Why did she borrow the necklace?
3. How did the loss change her life?
4. What is ironic about the ending?
