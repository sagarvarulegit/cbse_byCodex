# Physical Fitness and Wellness chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Fitness

Physical fitness means:

1. ability to perform daily activities efficiently
2. never moving
3. eating only one food

Answer: 1
Explanation: Fitness is the ability to do daily work efficiently.

## 2. Wellness

Wellness includes:

1. healthy body, mind, and habits
2. only marks in exams
3. only sleep

Answer: 1
Explanation: Wellness includes physical, mental, and lifestyle health.

## 3. Exercise

Regular exercise helps improve:

1. strength, stamina, and flexibility
2. laziness
3. disease spread

Answer: 1
Explanation: Exercise improves strength, stamina, and flexibility.

## 4. Rest

Rest is important because it:

1. helps recovery
2. damages fitness always
3. replaces food

Answer: 1
Explanation: Rest helps the body recover after activity.

## 5. Habit

A good wellness habit is:

1. balanced routine
2. skipping all activity
3. ignoring sleep

Answer: 1
Explanation: A balanced routine supports wellness.
