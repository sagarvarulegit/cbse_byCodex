Task: Research and rank the most important CBSE Class 10 topics for video production based on marks weightage and previous year board exam frequency.

## Objective

Find out which CBSE Class 10 topics we should create videos for next. Rank them by importance using two criteria:
1. **Marks weightage** — how many marks does this topic carry in the board exam?
2. **Previous year frequency** — how often has this topic appeared in board exams (2020-2026)?

## What to Research

Search the web for:
1. CBSE Class 10 2026 board exam pattern and marks distribution by chapter/subject
2. Previous year CBSE Class 10 board paper analysis (2020-2026) — which chapters/topics appeared most frequently
3. Chapter-wise marks weightage for Mathematics, Science, and Social Science
4. Most repeated question types (MCQ, 2-mark, 3-mark, 5-mark, case-based)
5. High-yield chapters that appear almost every year
