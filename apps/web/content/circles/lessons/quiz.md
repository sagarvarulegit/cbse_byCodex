# Circles chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Radius

A radius is a line segment from:

1. center to the circle
2. one point on the circle to another
3. the circle to a tangent only

## 2. Chord

A chord is:

1. a line through the center only
2. a segment joining two points on the circle
3. a line touching the circle at one point

## 3. Diameter

The diameter is:

1. twice the radius
2. half the radius
3. equal to the circumference

## 4. Tangent

A tangent touches a circle at:

1. two points
2. one point
3. no points

## 5. Secant

A secant is a line that:

1. does not meet the circle
2. meets the circle at two points
3. is always a radius
