## Accuracy decisions

- NCERT Figure 8.5 is preserved as `RRyy × rrYY`, not the more common
  `RRYY × rryy` example.
- F2 monohybrid genotype and phenotype ratios are separately labelled.
- The blood-group and light-eye questions are answered as “insufficient
  evidence,” matching the reasoning demanded by NCERT.
- Human sex determination is described as a random X- or Y-bearing sperm event;
  it never blames the mother.
- Activity 8.1 is presented as an observation exercise, not as proof of a simple
  dominant/recessive rule for human earlobes.
