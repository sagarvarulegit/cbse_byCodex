# 13.2.2 Managing the Garbage We Produce

## Biodegradable versus non-biodegradable

- Biodegradable: decomposed by specific microorganisms/enzymes under suitable
  conditions—food waste, paper, cotton, wood, leather.
- Non-biodegradable: not broken down biologically under ordinary environmental
  conditions or persists very long—many plastics, glass, metals, persistent
  chemicals.

Enzymes are substrate-specific; decomposers do not possess enzymes for every
human-made material.

## Effects

Biodegradable waste:

- returns nutrients when managed/composted;
- excessive unmanaged waste causes smell, disease vectors, methane, and oxygen
  depletion in water.

Non-biodegradable waste:

- accumulates and occupies land;
- pollutes soil/water and can injure/poison organisms;
- may enter food chains and biomagnify;
- burning may release toxic gases.

Even 100% biodegradable waste has an impact if generated faster than it
decomposes or dumped in one place.

## Waste hierarchy

1. **Refuse/reduce** unnecessary and single-use items.
2. **Reuse/repair** before replacement.
3. **Segregate** wet/biodegradable, dry recyclable, sanitary, hazardous, and
   e-waste at source.
4. **Compost/biogas** suitable organic waste.
5. **Recycle** clean recoverable material through authorised systems.
6. Dispose residual/hazardous/e-waste safely.

## Activities 13.5–13.9

- bury mixed samples under at least 15 cm soil, keep moist, inspect every 15
  days, and compare decomposition;
- research persistence and claims about biodegradable plastics;
- audit daily home/class waste and local collection/segregation;
- investigate sewage and industrial-waste treatment;
- investigate e-waste hazards and environmental costs of plastic recycling.

The disposable-cup example teaches life-cycle thinking: plastic persists;
large-scale clay cups remove fertile topsoil; paper has advantages but still
uses resources. Reusable cups usually avoid repeated disposal.

## Two-action answers

Carry reusable bags/bottles; segregate and compost wet waste; refuse
single-use packaging; repair/reuse; send recyclables and e-waste to authorised
collectors. Give any two with mechanisms.
