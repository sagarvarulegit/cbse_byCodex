# Agriculture chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Kharif crops

Kharif crops are mainly grown during:

1. monsoon season
2. winter only
3. summer vacation only

Answer: 1
Explanation: Kharif crops are sown and grown in the monsoon season.

## 2. Rabi crops

Rabi crops are mainly grown during:

1. winter season
2. rainy season only
3. no season

Answer: 1
Explanation: Rabi crops grow in the winter season.

## 3. Plantation

Tea is commonly grown as:

1. plantation crop
2. mineral crop
3. fish crop

Answer: 1
Explanation: Tea is a plantation crop grown on large estates.

## 4. Food crop

Rice and wheat are examples of:

1. food crops
2. fibres only
3. energy minerals

Answer: 1
Explanation: Rice and wheat are staple food crops.

## 5. Technology

Agricultural reforms help improve:

1. productivity and farming methods
2. only city traffic
3. only printing

Answer: 1
Explanation: Agricultural reforms aim to improve farming methods and output.
