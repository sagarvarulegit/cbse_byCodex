# The Thief's Story

## chapter goal

By the end of this chapter, a student should be able to:
- describe Hari Singh's transformation
- explain Anil's trust and generosity
- understand the conflict between theft and conscience
- answer questions on values, narration, and character change

## teacher note on textbook alignment

This pack follows `jefp102.pdf`: Hari Singh's meeting with Anil, his lies, Anil's trust, theft of money, inner conflict, return, and moral change.

## chapter flow for the platform

1. Hari Singh's background
2. Meeting Anil
3. Trust and education
4. Theft and escape
5. Inner conflict
6. Return and transformation

## 1) Hari Singh

Hari Singh is a young thief who changes his name often to escape the police and former employers.

He is clever, observant, and used to lying.

## 2) Anil

Anil is kind, simple, trusting, and generous.

He gives Hari food, shelter, and the chance to learn reading, writing, and arithmetic.

## 3) trust

Anil knows Hari takes small profits from shopping but does not punish him.

This trust begins to affect Hari's conscience.

## 4) theft

Hari steals Anil's money and runs away.

At the station, he realises that stealing the money means losing the chance to become educated and respected.

## 5) transformation

Hari returns the money and quietly places it back.

Anil seems to know what happened but does not expose him. His silence gives Hari a chance to change.

## exam-friendly summary notes

- Hari is a thief but not beyond reform.
- Anil's trust is the turning point.
- Education represents a better future.
- Hari returns because his conscience awakens.
- The story shows trust can transform a person.

## common mistakes students make

1. Saying Hari returns only because he is afraid.
2. Ignoring the importance of education.
3. Calling Anil foolish; his trust is morally powerful.

## board tips

- In transformation answers, mention conscience and education.
- Use first-person narration as a clue to Hari's inner conflict.
- Explain Anil's silence as kindness.

## short answer practice

1. Why did Hari Singh lie about his name?
2. What did Anil offer Hari besides food?
3. Why did Hari return the money?
4. How did Anil respond the next morning?

## mini quiz with answers

3. Hari steals money from under the mattress.
4. Hari returns because his conscience changes.
5. Trust is the main force of reform.
