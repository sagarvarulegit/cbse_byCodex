# Coordinate Geometry chapter quiz

## 1. Distance formula

The distance between $(x_1,y_1)$ and $(x_2,y_2)$ is:

1. $\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}$
2. $(x_2-x_1)+(y_2-y_1)$
3. $\sqrt{x_1x_2+y_1y_2}$
4. $|x_1+x_2|+|y_1+y_2|$

Answer: 1
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 7.2 aligned
Explanation: Horizontal and vertical differences form the two legs of a right triangle.

## 2. Distance from the origin

The distance of $(6,8)$ from the origin is:

1. $7$
2. $10$
3. $12$
4. $14$

Answer: 2
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 7.2 aligned
Explanation: $\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=\sqrt{100}=10$.

## 3. Midpoint

The midpoint of $(-2,5)$ and $(6,1)$ is:

1. $(2,3)$
2. $(4,6)$
3. $(-4,4)$
4. $(2,2)$

Answer: 1
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 7.3 aligned
Explanation: Average the corresponding coordinates: $((-2+6)/2,(5+1)/2)=(2,3)$.

## 4. Internal section formula

A point divides the segment joining $(1,2)$ and $(7,8)$ internally in the ratio $2:1$. Its coordinates are:

1. $(3,4)$
2. $(4,5)$
3. $(5,6)$
4. $(6,7)$

Answer: 3
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Section 7.3 aligned
Explanation: $((2\cdot7+1\cdot1)/3,(2\cdot8+1\cdot2)/3)=(5,6)$.

## 5. Point on the y-axis

Every point on the $y$-axis has:

1. $x=0$
2. $y=0$
3. $x=y$
4. $x=1$

Answer: 1
Topic: core-notes
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 7.1 aligned
Explanation: The $y$-axis is the vertical line whose $x$-coordinate is zero.

## 6. Equidistant points

Which point is equidistant from $(0,0)$ and $(4,0)$?

1. $(0,2)$
2. $(2,3)$
3. $(4,2)$
4. $(1,0)$

Answer: 2
Topic: ncert-exercises-and-revision
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Exercise 7.1 aligned
Explanation: Its distances are both $\sqrt{13}$; it lies on the perpendicular bisector $x=2$.
