# Metals and Non-metals chapter quiz

Answer all 20 questions after completing the seven canonical chapter lessons.

## 1. The sheet property

Which property allows aluminium to be rolled into foil?

1. ductility
2. sonority
3. malleability
4. brittleness

Answer: 3
Topic: physical-properties
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Activity 3.3 aligned
Explanation: Malleability is the ability of a metal to be beaten or rolled into thin sheets.

## 2. Physical-property exception

Which statement is correct?

1. Iodine is a lustrous non-metal
2. Mercury is a gaseous metal
3. Graphite is a metal
4. Bromine is a liquid metal

Answer: 1
Topic: thermal-conductivity-and-conductivity
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 3.1.2 exceptions aligned
Explanation: Iodine is a non-metal but has lustre. Mercury is a liquid metal, graphite is a conducting non-metal and bromine is a liquid non-metal.

## 3. Activity 3.5

Why does the wax-held pin fall when the metal wire is heated near its clamped
end?

1. The wire burns and releases gas
2. Heat conducts along the wire and melts the wax
3. The pin becomes magnetic
4. The wax conducts electricity

Answer: 2
Topic: thermal-conductivity-and-conductivity
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Activity 3.5 and official 2025-26 SQP Q25 aligned
Explanation: Metal conducts heat from the hot region to the wax; the wire itself does not melt in the activity.

## 4. Amphoteric evidence

Which pair of reactions proves that aluminium oxide is amphoteric?

1. It reacts with oxygen and water
2. It reacts with both HCl and NaOH
3. It reacts with copper and iron
4. It dissolves only in water

Answer: 2
Topic: oxides-and-chemical-properties
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Section 3.2.1 and 2023-2025 board-demand aligned
Explanation: Amphoteric oxides react with both acids and bases to form salts and water.

## 5. Copper in air

What happens when copper is heated in air?

1. It burns with a white flame and forms Cu2O
2. It forms a black CuO coating
3. It forms green FeSO4
4. It does not change at all

Answer: 2
Topic: oxides-and-chemical-properties
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 3.2.1 and 2023 board case aligned
Explanation: `2Cu + O2 -> 2CuO`; copper(II) oxide is black.

## 6. Metal with water

Which metal does not react with cold water but reacts with hot water?

1. sodium
2. calcium
3. magnesium
4. copper

Answer: 3
Topic: reactivity-series-and-displacement
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Section 3.2.2 aligned
Explanation: Magnesium reacts with hot water to form magnesium hydroxide and hydrogen.

## 7. Calcium floats

Calcium starts floating during its reaction with water because:

1. calcium is less dense than every liquid
2. oxygen bubbles stick to it
3. hydrogen bubbles stick to its surface
4. calcium hydroxide is a gas

Answer: 3
Topic: reactivity-series-and-displacement
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Section 3.2.2 and 2024 board-demand aligned
Explanation: Hydrogen bubbles formed in the reaction adhere to the calcium surface and make it float.

## 8. Nitric acid exception

Why is hydrogen generally not evolved when a metal reacts with nitric acid?

1. Nitric acid contains no hydrogen
2. Nitric acid oxidises the produced hydrogen to water
3. Every metal lies below hydrogen
4. Nitric acid is a weak base

Answer: 2
Topic: reactivity-series-and-displacement
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Section 3.2.3 and 2024-2026 board-demand aligned
Explanation: HNO3 is a strong oxidising agent; magnesium and manganese with very dilute HNO3 are textbook exceptions.

## 9. Iron and copper sulphate

Which complete observation is correct when an iron nail is placed in copper
sulphate solution?

1. No reaction
2. Blue solution becomes pale green and copper coats the nail
3. Pale-green solution becomes blue and iron coats the nail
4. Hydrogen gas alone forms

Answer: 2
Topic: reactivity-series-and-displacement
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Activity 3.12 aligned
Explanation: Iron is more reactive than copper: `Fe + CuSO4 -> FeSO4 + Cu`.

## 10. Electron transfer

Why is the formula of magnesium chloride `MgCl2`?

1. Mg gains one electron and Cl loses two
2. Mg loses two electrons and each of two Cl atoms gains one
3. Mg and Cl share four electrons equally
4. Chlorine forms a 2+ ion

Answer: 2
Topic: ionic-compounds
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Figure 3.6 aligned
Explanation: One Mg2+ requires two Cl- ions for electrical neutrality.

## 11. Solid versus molten

Solid sodium chloride does not conduct electricity, but molten sodium chloride
does because:

1. solid sodium chloride contains no ions
2. molten sodium chloride contains free electrons from metal
3. ions are fixed in the solid and mobile in the melt
4. melting converts sodium chloride into copper

Answer: 3
Topic: ionic-compounds
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Section 3.3.1 aligned
Explanation: Charge is carried by mobile ions in molten or aqueous ionic compounds.

## 12. High melting point

Ionic compounds have high melting points mainly because:

1. they contain weak molecular attraction
2. strong electrostatic attraction holds oppositely charged ions
3. all ionic compounds contain water
4. their atoms are uncharged and far apart

Answer: 2
Topic: ionic-compounds
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Section 3.3.1 and 2026 board Q25 aligned
Explanation: Considerable energy is required to overcome strong inter-ionic attraction.

## 13. Mineral and ore

Which statement is correct?

1. Every mineral is an ore
2. Every ore is a mineral, but not every mineral is an ore
3. Gangue is the pure metal
4. Ore contains no impurity

Answer: 2
Topic: corrosion-and-prevention
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Section 3.4 aligned
Explanation: An ore contains enough metal for profitable extraction; gangue is unwanted impurity.

## 14. Roasting and calcination

Which pairing is correct?

1. sulphide ore—calcination in excess air
2. carbonate ore—roasting in excess air
3. sulphide ore—roasting in excess air
4. carbonate ore—electrolysis only

Answer: 3
Topic: corrosion-and-prevention
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Section 3.4.4 and 2023/2026 board-demand aligned
Explanation: Sulphides are roasted in excess air; carbonates are calcined in limited or no air.

## 15. High-activity extraction

Why is sodium extracted by electrolytic reduction rather than carbon
reduction?

1. Sodium is below carbon in reactivity
2. Sodium has greater affinity for oxygen than carbon
3. Sodium chloride is a covalent liquid
4. Carbon is an ionic metal

Answer: 2
Topic: corrosion-and-prevention
Difficulty: hard
Source: NCERT Section 3.4.5 and official 2025-26 SQP Q26 aligned
Explanation: Highly reactive metals cannot be displaced from their stable compounds by carbon and require electrolysis.

## 16. Thermit reaction

What is the main useful result of `Fe2O3 + 2Al -> 2Fe + Al2O3 + heat`?

1. cold iron powder
2. molten iron that can join railway tracks
3. hydrogen for balloons
4. sodium metal

Answer: 2
Topic: corrosion-and-prevention
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Figure 3.11 and 2023-2026 board-demand aligned
Explanation: The highly exothermic displacement produces molten iron used for welding heavy iron parts.

## 17. Copper refining

Which setup is correct in electrolytic refining of copper?

1. pure copper anode; impure copper cathode
2. impure copper anode; pure copper cathode
3. carbon anode; sodium cathode
4. both electrodes are iron

Answer: 2
Topic: corrosion-and-prevention
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Figure 3.12 and 2026 board Q28 aligned
Explanation: Impure copper dissolves from the anode and pure copper deposits on the cathode; insoluble impurities form anode mud.

## 18. Rusting control

4. all tubes equally

Answer: 1
Topic: corrosion-and-prevention
Difficulty: easy
Source: NCERT Activity 3.14 and 2025 board Q30 aligned
Explanation: Rusting requires both oxygen and water.

## 19. Gold purity

What does 22-carat gold mean in the NCERT description?

1. 22 parts gold and 2 parts copper or silver
2. 22 parts copper and 2 parts gold
3. completely pure gold
4. gold mixed only with mercury

Answer: 1
Topic: corrosion-and-prevention
Difficulty: medium
Source: NCERT Section 3.5.1 Do You Know aligned
Explanation: Pure 24-carat gold is soft, so jewellery commonly uses 22 parts gold alloyed with 2 parts copper or silver.

## 20. Copper hot-water tank

Why is copper preferred over steel for hot-water tanks?

1. Copper reacts violently with steam
2. Copper is less reactive and does not react with water or steam
3. Steel is a non-metal
4. Copper is always cheaper than steel

Answer: 2
Topic: ncert-exercises-and-revision
Difficulty: hard
Source: NCERT Exercise 16 aligned
Explanation: Iron in steel can react with steam and rust, while copper lies below hydrogen and does not react with water under these conditions.
