# Coordinate Geometry chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Axes

The horizontal axis is the:

1. y-axis
2. x-axis
3. origin

Answer: 2
Explanation: The horizontal axis is the x-axis, not the y-axis.

## 2. Quadrants

The point `(2, 3)` lies in:

1. first quadrant
2. second quadrant
3. third quadrant

Answer: 1
Explanation: Positive x and positive y place the point in the first quadrant.

## 3. Origin

The coordinates of the origin are:

1. `(0, 0)`
2. `(1, 1)`
3. `(-1, -1)`

Answer: 1
Explanation: The origin is the point where both coordinates are zero.

## 4. Distance idea

Coordinate geometry is used to study:

1. points, lines, and shapes on a plane
2. only arithmetic sums
3. only triangles with no coordinates

Answer: 1
Explanation: Coordinate geometry studies geometric objects using coordinates on a plane.

## 5. Sign rule

In the second quadrant, `x` is:

1. positive and `y` negative
2. negative and `y` positive
3. both positive

Answer: 2
Explanation: In the second quadrant, x is negative and y is positive.
