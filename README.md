# ChapterReady - CBSE Class 10 Learning Portal

Product and architecture for the CBSE Class 10 online learning platform across Android, iOS, and Web.

## Overview
ChapterReady is a unified 10-section learning system focused on helping students master Class 10 subjects one chapter at a time. The platform features an intent-first architecture ("Learn a Chapter", "Practice Questions", "Prepare for Boards") and follows a 5-Step Method: Pick a Chapter → Learn → Practice → Master → Revise → Board Ready.

## Key Features & Milestones
- **Interactive Learning & Virtual Labs:** Explore grid linking Virtual Labs & Diagrams, Formula Cheatsheets, Arcade & Formula Rush, and AI Coach.
- **Dedicated AI Coach:** Socratic explanations and 1-click tutor activation.
- **AI Evaluator (Milestone 4):** Grounded AI Snap & Grade Written Evaluator for handwritten answers.
- **Mock Exams & Practice (Milestones 2 & 3):** Exemplar HOTS Arena, PYQ Drill ladders, Hindi Mock Series, and Mathematics Basic (241) Mock Series with CBSE Step-Marking Rubrics.
- **Knowledge Base Integration:** Relies on the `cbse_KB` repository for a clean pipeline of marking schemes, exemplars, and PYQs (Milestone 1).
- **Extensive Cheatsheets:** 27-Chapter Formula & Reaction Cheatsheet Pack covering Mathematics and Science.
