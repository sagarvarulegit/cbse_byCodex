# The Thief's Story chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Hari Singh

Hari Singh is a:

1. young thief
2. shopkeeper
3. teacher

Answer: 1
Explanation: Hari Singh is a young thief who changes his life after meeting Anil.

## 2. Anil

Anil is:

1. kind and trusting
2. angry and cruel
3. suspicious and rude

Answer: 1
Explanation: Anil treats Hari Singh kindly and trusts him, even when Hari Singh is not honest at first.

## 3. Trust

Hari Singh changes because of:

1. kindness and education
2. punishment and fear
3. travel and weather

Answer: 1
Explanation: Anil's kindness and habit of teaching Hari Singh slowly make him want to improve.

## 4. Theft

At the end, Hari Singh:

1. returns and stays
2. leaves with money but returns to Anil's trust
3. becomes a policeman

Answer: 2
Explanation: He steals the money, but he comes back and chooses Anil's trust and education over the theft.

## 5. Theme

The story values:

1. trust and education
2. cheating and greed
3. silence and fear

Answer: 1
Explanation: The story shows that trust and education can change a person for the better.

Recheck Hari Singh, Anil, and the theme of reform before retrying.
