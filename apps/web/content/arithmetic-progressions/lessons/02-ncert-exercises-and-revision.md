# NCERT Exercises and Chapter Revision

## Exercise 5.1

The four-question set checks whether situations and number lists form APs, asks for $a$ and $d$, and asks for the next terms.

For every list, write at least three consecutive differences. If they are not equal, state "not an AP" and stop.

## Exercise 5.2

This is the full nth-term workshop. Its printed tasks cover:

- filling missing $a,d,n,a_n$ values
- selecting and justifying an answer choice
- completing pattern boxes
- finding a requested term or number of terms
- deciding whether a number belongs to an AP
- terms counted from the end
- finding a missing term, common difference or parameter
- divisibility and monthly-installment applications

Useful textbook results include:

- 30th term of $10,7,4,\ldots$ is $-77$
- number of terms in $18,15\frac12,13,\ldots,-47$ is $27$
- $-150$ is not a term of $11,8,5,2,\ldots$
- 31st term of the AP whose 11th and 16th terms are $38$ and $73$ is $178$
- 29th term of the 50-term AP whose 3rd term is $12$ and last term is $106$ is $64$
- the term $132$ greater than the 54th term of $3,15,27,39,\ldots$ is the 65th term

## Exercise 5.3

The eighteen-question set practises $S_n$, missing AP data and applications.

Use this order:

1. identify $a,d,n$ or $l$
2. choose $S_n=\frac n2[2a+(n-1)d]$ or $S_n=\frac n2(a+l)$
3. solve any resulting equation for $n$
4. keep only admissible positive integer values

The applications include construction penalties, prize money, tree planting, spiral semicircles, log stacks and a potato race. Figures 5.4-5.6 carry the geometry and distance information; do not replace them with guessed values.

## Exercise 5.4 (Optional)

The five problems on negative terms, product conditions, ladder rungs, house numbers and stadium steps are marked optional and not from the examination point of view. They may be used as enrichment, not as core board coverage.

## Common mistakes

- rounding a non-integer value of $n$ instead of rejecting it
- forgetting that a decreasing AP has negative $d$
- summing distances when the question asks for a single term

## Board-answer checklist

- Formula selected for the information given
- $a$, $d$, $n$ and $l$ labelled
- Units and interpretation in the final line

## One-minute revision

AP questions are either position questions, total questions, or a combination of both.

## Comic recap

- Pattern: "Check my differences first."
- Position: "Use $a+(n-1)d$."
- Total: "Use the correct $S_n$ form."
- Application: "Translate the result back into the story."
