# Words and Expressions Unit 3 - Two Stories about Flying

## chapter goal

By the end of this workbook unit, a student should be able to:
- answer MCQs based on "His First Flight" and "The Black Aeroplane"
- create comprehension questions
- discuss fear, courage, and mystery
- practise vocabulary, grammar, editing, speaking, writing, and project tasks

## teacher note on textbook alignment

This pack follows `jewe203.pdf`: MCQs on the young seagull, question-making for "The Black Aeroplane", comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, editing, listening/speaking, writing, and project work.

## chapter flow for the platform

1. MCQ reading check
2. Question creation
3. Fear and courage discussion
4. Mystery reading
5. Vocabulary
6. Grammar and editing
7. Writing and project work

## 1) MCQ skill

Read all options before choosing.

For story MCQs, return to the exact event: the seagull is afraid, hungry, tempted by food, and then flies.

## 2) creating questions

The unit asks students to make MCQs on "The Black Aeroplane".

A good MCQ has one correct answer, three believable distractors, and clear wording.

## 3) fear and courage

Use the seagull story to discuss how fear is overcome through action.

Use the pilot story to discuss calmness and help in danger.

## 4) mystery reading

Mystery questions often ask what is known, what is unknown, and what clues create suspense.

Do not force a final explanation when the story keeps the helper mysterious.

## exam-friendly summary notes

- MCQs need close reading.
- Good questions are clear and fair.
- Courage means acting despite fear.
- Mystery depends on clues and gaps.
- Writing should compare the two stories when asked.

## common mistakes students make

1. Choosing MCQ options without checking the text.
2. Writing two correct answers in a self-made MCQ.
3. Explaining the black aeroplane with unsupported certainty.

## board tips

- Underline key words in MCQs.
- In question-making, avoid confusing wording.
- For compare answers, use both stories.

## short answer practice

1. What makes a good MCQ?
2. Why was the young seagull afraid?
3. What creates mystery in the pilot story?
4. How can fear lead to courage?

## mini quiz with answers

1. The seagull flies when hunger pushes him.
2. The black aeroplane remains mysterious.
3. MCQs should have one correct answer.
4. Distractors are wrong but believable options.
5. Both stories involve danger and courage.
