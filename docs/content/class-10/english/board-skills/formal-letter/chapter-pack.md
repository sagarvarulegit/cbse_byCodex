# Writing Skills - Formal Letter

## chapter goal

Write a formal letter that is clear, polite, correctly formatted, and based on the given situation.

## teacher note on textbook alignment

This pack supports the current English writing-skill domain for a formal letter based on a situation.

## chapter flow for the platform

1. Understand the situation.
2. Use the correct format.
3. Keep tone formal.
4. Organise the body.
5. Revise for accuracy.

## 1) purpose first

Before writing, identify who you are, who receives the letter, and what action is needed.

The purpose controls tone and content.

## 2) format

A formal letter normally includes sender address, date, receiver address, subject, salutation, body, closing, and name.

Use the format your school teacher has approved.

## 3) body structure

Paragraph 1 states the purpose.

Paragraph 2 gives necessary details, reasons, facts, or problem explanation.

Paragraph 3 requests action or closes politely.

## 4) tone

Be polite and direct. Do not sound casual, angry, dramatic, or emotional.

Formal writing should be respectful even when it reports a problem.

## exam-friendly summary notes

- Identify sender, receiver, purpose, and action.
- Format matters.
- Body should be organised in clear paragraphs.
- Tone must stay formal.
- Do not add irrelevant story details.

## common mistakes students make

1. Forgetting the subject line.
2. Writing like a friendly chat.
3. Giving too many emotional details and no clear request.

## board tips

- Underline the task purpose in the prompt.
- Use short paragraphs.
- End with a clear request, suggestion, or expected action.
