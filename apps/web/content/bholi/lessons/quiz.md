# Bholi chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Bholi

Bholi's real name is:

1. Sulekha
2. Rani
3. Kiran

Answer: 1
Explanation: Bholi is the nickname, and Sulekha is her real name in the story.

## 2. Confidence

Bholi grows through:

1. education and support
2. fear and silence
3. punishment only

Answer: 1
Explanation: Her confidence grows because of school, kindness, and encouragement from her teacher.

## 3. Stammering

The story focuses on:

1. her lack of confidence and stammering
2. her fame in sports
3. her travel journey

Answer: 1
Explanation: The story shows how Bholi begins as a shy girl who stammers and slowly gains courage.

## 4. Teacher

The teacher encourages Bholi by:

1. giving her confidence
2. scolding her for fun
3. ignoring her

Answer: 1
Explanation: The teacher treats Bholi kindly and helps her believe that she can speak and study with confidence.

## 5. Marriage

Bholi refuses the marriage because:

1. the groom is cruel and greedy
2. she forgets the date
3. she wants a bus ride

Answer: 1
Explanation: She sees the groom's greed and cruelty, so she refuses to accept a bad marriage.
