# Metals and Non-metals chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Metal

Metals are usually:

1. dull and brittle
2. lustrous and malleable
3. gases at room temperature

Answer: 2
Explanation: Metals are usually lustrous and malleable.

## 2. Non-metal

Non-metals are usually:

1. good conductors of heat and electricity
2. poor conductors
3. always shiny and malleable

Answer: 2
Explanation: Non-metals are generally poor conductors.

## 3. Reactivity series

The reactivity series arranges metals by:

1. density
2. tendency to react
3. colour

Answer: 2
Explanation: The reactivity series orders metals by how readily they react.

## 4. Ionic compound

Ionic compounds are formed by:

1. transfer of electrons
2. sharing of sentences
3. melting only

Answer: 1
Explanation: Ionic compounds form when electrons are transferred between atoms.

## 5. Corrosion

Rusting is an example of:

1. corrosion
2. distillation
3. condensation

Answer: 1
Explanation: Rusting is a common example of corrosion.
