# Words and Expressions Unit 6 - Mijbil the Otter

## chapter goal

By the end of this workbook unit, a student should be able to:
- discuss responsible pet care
- describe Mijbil's actions and emotions
- read wildlife-related passages with empathy
- practise vocabulary, grammar, editing, speaking, writing, and project work

## teacher note on textbook alignment

This pack follows `jewe206.pdf`: discussion on care for Mijbil, matching actions with feelings, reading comprehension on a rescued civet cat, vocabulary, grammar, editing, listening/speaking, writing, and project work.

## chapter flow for the platform

1. Pet care discussion
2. Mijbil's actions and feelings
3. Wildlife rescue reading
4. Vocabulary of animals and care
5. Grammar and editing
6. Speaking and writing
7. Project work

## 1) pet care

The unit asks students to think about the author's commitment to Mijbil.

Responsible care includes food, exercise, space, safety, observation, and affection.

## 2) actions and feelings

Students connect what Mijbil does with how he feels.

This builds inference: actions like plunging into water show joy and natural instinct.

## 3) wildlife reading

The comprehension passage broadens the theme from pet care to wildlife rescue.

Read for facts, care practices, and the relationship between humans and animals.

## 4) vocabulary

Important vocabulary includes animal habits, rescue, rehabilitation, nocturnal, arboreal, and omnivorous.

Learn meanings through context and examples.

## 5) writing

Writing tasks may include animal descriptions, care instructions, reports, or personal reflections.

Use observation-based details instead of vague affection.

## exam-friendly summary notes

- Pet care means commitment.
- Mijbil's behaviour shows emotion and instinct.
- Wildlife rescue requires knowledge and patience.
- Vocabulary should be linked to animal behaviour.
- Good writing uses observed actions.

## common mistakes students make

1. Writing only "I love animals" without specific care points.
2. Confusing pet care with keeping animals confined.
3. Ignoring vocabulary from the reading passage.

## board tips

- In animal writing, use action verbs.
- For care answers, mention food, space, health, and exercise.
- In comprehension, separate fact from feeling.

## short answer practice

1. What arrangements did the author make for Mijbil?
2. How do actions reveal an animal's feelings?
3. What is wildlife rehabilitation?
4. What details improve animal description?
