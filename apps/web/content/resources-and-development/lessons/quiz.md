# Resources and Development chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Resource

A resource is something that:

1. satisfies human needs and is usable
2. has no use at all
3. is always imported

Answer: 1
Explanation: A resource is useful and can satisfy human needs.

## 2. Sustainable development

Sustainable development means:

1. using resources without harming future needs
2. using everything at once
3. stopping all development

Answer: 1
Explanation: Sustainable development protects resources for future generations.

## 3. Resource planning

Resource planning is important because:

1. resources are unevenly distributed
2. every place has the same resources
3. planning is never needed

Answer: 1
Explanation: Resources are not evenly spread, so planning is necessary.

## 4. Land degradation

Land degradation means:

1. decline in land quality
2. increase in rainfall only
3. growth of all crops automatically

Answer: 1
Explanation: Land degradation is a decline in the quality of land.

## 5. Soil

Soil is important mainly for:

1. agriculture and ecosystems
2. only transport
3. only elections

Answer: 1
Explanation: Soil supports farming and helps ecosystems function.
