# Footprints without Feet

## chapter goal

By the end of this chapter, a student should be able to:
- explain Griffin's invisibility and misuse of science
- describe the sequence of strange events
- understand mystery, humour, and moral warning
- answer questions on scientific responsibility

## teacher note on textbook alignment

This pack follows `jefp105.pdf`: muddy footprints, Griffin's invisibility, London store episode, village inn episode, thefts, violence, and escape.

## chapter flow for the platform

1. Strange footprints
2. Griffin's discovery
3. Misuse of invisibility
4. London store episode
5. Iping village events
6. Moral warning

## 1) strange footprints

Two boys see muddy footprints appearing without a visible person.

The mystery is explained by Griffin, an invisible scientist.

## 2) Griffin's discovery

Griffin discovers a way to make the human body invisible.

His scientific achievement is brilliant, but his character is lawless.

## 3) misuse of science

Instead of using discovery for good, Griffin uses it for revenge, theft, and escape.

He sets fire to his landlord's house and steals clothes, food, and money.

## 4) London store

Griffin enters a store for warmth, eats, wears clothes, and sleeps.

When workers arrive, he escapes by becoming invisible again.

## 5) Iping village

At the inn, Griffin's strange behaviour creates suspicion.

When people try to investigate, he uses invisibility to frighten and attack them.

## 6) message

Science without morality can become dangerous.

The story warns that intelligence must be guided by responsibility.

## exam-friendly summary notes

- Griffin is invisible but not noble.
- He misuses science for selfish purposes.
- The footprints create mystery.
- The story mixes science fiction with moral warning.
- Griffin's lawlessness makes him dangerous.

## common mistakes students make

1. Treating Griffin as a hero just because he is intelligent.
2. Forgetting his crimes.
3. Writing invisibility as magic instead of scientific discovery in the story.

## board tips

- In character answers, contrast brilliant scientist and lawless person.
- Use examples of misuse: arson, theft, violence.
- Explain the title through visible footprints of an invisible man.

## short answer practice

1. Why were the boys surprised by the footprints?
2. How did Griffin become invisible?
3. How did he misuse his discovery?
4. What message does the story give?

## mini quiz with answers

1. Griffin is a scientist.
2. He becomes invisible through experiments and drugs.
3. He enters a London store for warmth.
4. He steals and frightens people.
5. The story warns against irresponsible science.
