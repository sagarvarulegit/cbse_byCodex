# Curriculum and Content Memory

Read this file for curriculum registry, lesson publication, textbook-derived notes, chapter fidelity, and board-evidence work. Also read `NOTES_GUIDELINES_STANDARDS.md` before changing student content.

## Curriculum registry and coverage

- Curriculum source of truth for Class 10 subject definitions lives under `apps/web/lib/curriculum`, with session-aware sources in `official-sources.ts`, active lookups in `registry.ts`, validation in `validation.ts`, coverage formulas in `coverage.ts`, and per-subject data in `subjects/class-10/*.ts`.
- Curriculum session history is structurally immutable: each subject definition imports an explicit per-session module such as `apps/web/lib/curriculum/sessions/2026-27.ts`, while `registry.ts` separately selects the active session. Official-source records keep their own session and verification date.
- Historical sample papers and board paper archives can support comparison, but they must stay explicitly historical and cannot silently define the active blueprint for the current session.
- Official source eligibility is explicit through source role, subject-code applicability, session, source type, `mayDriveCurrentCurriculum`, and `mayDriveCurrentBlueprint`. Science, English, and Hindi remain available current subjects while their 2026-27 blueprint readiness is blocked because the registered PDFs embed 2025-26 paper-design evidence.
- Core curriculum coverage is topic-level. `CurriculumTopic.platformContentRefs` maps official topic IDs to chapter-pack or exact lesson evidence; board coverage ignores internal/formative, project-only, periodic-only, supporting-resource, and excluded topics even when they share a platform pack.
- Coverage dimensions use `complete`, `incomplete`, `not-configured`, and `not-applicable`. Empty denominators or absent publication/question evidence never report 100%, and board-paper seed readiness is not applicable to internal HPE.
- HPE follows the official internal structure and weighting: Games / Sports 50, Health and Fitness 25, SEWA 25, and Health and Activity Card 0. Existing packs are mapped as internal topics or supporting resources rather than board chapters.
- Words and Expressions - II follows the current local NCERT Reprint 2026-27 workbook source: it has 9 units. Units 1-4 and 7-9 are supporting resources, Units 5-6 are excluded, and the earlier Unit 10-11 placeholders must stay out of the active registry because they belong to an older workbook shape.
- Mathematics Basic is a hidden `future` subject until a separate activation review is completed.
- Subject pages render official curriculum chapter titles, scopes, and topic scopes from the registry; legacy `course-content.ts` topic strings are platform metadata, not official 2026-27 scope.
- The student subject pages consume the registry instead of hard-coded subject lists: `apps/web/app/subjects/page.tsx` uses `getVisibleCurriculumSubjects()` plus `buildSubjectCatalog()`, and `apps/web/app/subjects/[slug]/page.tsx` resolves a registry subject before rendering mapped chapter routes.
- Broad library availability is derived from one catalog classifier and must remain explicit: `lesson-wise` means at least one published lesson file, `revision-pack` means pack fallback only, and `coming-soon` means neither is available. Lesson-wise takes precedence when both sources exist.
- The 2026-08-04 active inventory is Maths 14/14 lesson-wise, Science 13/13 lesson-wise, Social Science 22/22 revision packs, English 1 lesson-wise plus 39 revision packs, Hindi 27 revision packs, and HPE 0/5 available. These are availability counts, not claims that every item is source-reviewed or pilot-ready.

## Lesson publication and chapter navigation

- Lesson-wise chapter content is live only when a catalog lesson has a non-empty `fileName` and its chapter directory is registered in `lessonDirectories`. UI counts and previous/next navigation use available lessons only, while unpublished catalog lessons remain visible as Coming soon.
- Student chapter selection opens `/chapters/<slug>/notes` as one continuous reading view. The route loads every available lesson in catalog order, derives its contents list from exact numbered H2/H3 textbook headings, prefixes anchors per lesson to avoid duplicate support-section ids, and falls back to a chapter pack when lesson-wise content is unavailable.
- Pilot chapter entry points should make the complete continuous notes route the primary CTA, while the chapter contents preserves exact lesson-level textbook labels so the free pilot still feels guided instead of catalog-like.
- Chapter content QA uses `docs/content-style-qa-checklist.md`; pilot-ready chapters should pass easy scanning, short sections, example-after-concept, memory trick where useful, common mistake, board tip, practice prompt, and recap checks before wider access.
- Registering an assessment supplement as a live lesson expands `getAvailableLessons()`, previous/next navigation, visible lesson counts, total chapter duration, and the set of slugs accepted by strict quiz parsing. Update those consumers together, but define concept-quiz coverage against its intended core lesson set rather than assuming every live supplement needs a quiz topic.

## Social Science chapter packs

- Class 10 Social Science has 22 canonical NCERT chapter packs across History, Geography, Political Science and Economics. Their maintained source maps are the four `source-fidelity-tracker.md` files under `docs/content/class-10/social-science/<book>/`, using the local NCERT `Reprint 2026-27` PDFs in `textbooks/socialscience-*`.
- Social Science textbook headings are generally named rather than decimal-numbered. Preserve the printed heading order and wording; do not invent `1.1`, `2.1`, and similar labels when the book does not print them.
- Five old pack slugs were excluded duplicates and must stay canonical in live content: `rise-of-nationalism-in-europe`, `making-of-a-global-world`, `print-culture`, `sectors-of-indian-economy`, and `globalisation`. The older `the-*` or expanded variants belong only to the registry's excluded duplicate mappings.
- Social Science notes currently publish through the chapter-pack fallback, while their pack status remains `draft` until focused browser review. Quiz metadata and topic/source coverage are a separate assessment gate and must not be inferred from chapter-pack source fidelity.

## Science Chapter 1 reference implementation

- Chemical Reactions and Equations is the second lesson-wise pilot chapter after Real Numbers. It has six canonical NCERT lesson rows plus three unnumbered board-exam supplements, all driven by `apps/web/lib/course-content.ts` and markdown under `apps/web/content/chemical-reactions-and-equations/lessons/`. Keep the textbook path and supplement boundary explicit while treating all nine rows as live for navigation.
- Chapter 1 historical board practice is governed by `docs/content/class-10/science/chemical-reactions-and-equations/board-exam-evidence.md`. Extracted datasets are discovery aids only; quote a previous-year question only after visual comparison with the original PDF page. Preserve year, paper code, question number and printed page, call non-official scoring help `Platform marking points`, and never let historical frequencies silently define the current blueprint.
- Chapter 1 guided duration is 334 minutes (displayed as about 5 hr 35 min): Lesson 2 includes four independent workbench equations, Lesson 3 includes five activity interpretations, Lesson 6 includes all twenty NCERT exercises, and Lesson 7 includes twelve constructed responses. Do not reduce these estimates to reading time alone.
- On 26 July 2026, the newest published CBSE main-subject Class X Science SQP was still the official 2025-26 SQP. Chapter 1 maps every Chapter 1 or mixed-Chapter 1 demand in that SQP and includes five clearly labelled original pattern-transfer questions: redox with balancing, displacement comparison, electrolysis diagnosis, corrosion/material evaluation, and neutralisation as double displacement without precipitation. The two cross-chapter bridges remain visibly labelled as board extensions rather than NCERT sections. Recheck the official Class X SQP page before each session/year update and never relabel a platform variant as a PYQ.
- The user-supplied `science_chapter1_questions.md` is a 23-item secondary coverage bank, not sufficient provenance by itself: most entries lack exact paper code, question number and printed page. Its Q1-Q23 demands map individually in `board-exam-evidence.md`, while only the nine page- and marking-scheme-verified questions are published as PYQs. Dark storage of silver chloride and chlorine displacing iodine are taught as visibly labelled board applications, not invented NCERT content.
- The nine Chapter 1 PYQ answer guides were checked against official CBSE Class X Science marking-scheme archives for 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026 on 26 July 2026. Every official value point is represented. Keep explicit scoring phrases such as `endothermic decomposition`, `exothermic combination`, and `turns milky white`; do not assume an equation communicates a separately awarded classification or observation.
- Chapter 1 electrolysis board readiness goes beyond the NCERT volume observation: dilute sulphuric acid improves conductivity, hydrogen forms at the cathode, oxygen at the anode, cathode:anode volume is `2:1`, and cathode:anode mass is `1:8`. Historical papers also require unfamiliar reducing-agent transfer, so one memorised `CuO + H₂` example is insufficient.
- Chapter 1 equation fidelity must be checked as number-to-equation mappings: `(1.3)` is zinc with sulphuric acid, `(1.20)` is calcium carbonate decomposition, `(1.21)` is lead nitrate decomposition, water electrolysis is unnumbered, and `(1.30)` is the annotated copper oxide-hydrogen redox reaction. Activity 1.2 has no numbered NCERT figure; Figure 1.2 belongs to Activity 1.3.
- Science Chapter 1 is the platform's first gold-standard reference implementation. The reusable process lives in `docs/content/gold-standard-chapter-workflow.md`; reuse its evidence stages and quality gates, but rebuild each chapter map from its own current textbook instead of copying Chapter 1's lesson count or shape.
- Chapter 1's NCERT Group Activity belongs after Exercise 20. Keep Exercises 1-20 inside one uninterrupted textbook Questions block so every exercise answer remains behind the try-first disclosure UI.
