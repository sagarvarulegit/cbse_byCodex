# Words and Expressions Unit 1 - A Letter to God

## chapter goal

By the end of this workbook unit, a student should be able to:
- sequence events from "A Letter to God"
- discuss faith, trust, and human kindness
- practise reading comprehension linked with rural life and values
- use vocabulary in context
- complete grammar, editing, speaking, writing, and project tasks

## teacher note on textbook alignment

This pack follows `jewe201.pdf`: unit discussion on Lencho, jumbled story sequencing, reading comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, editing, listening/speaking, writing, and project work.

## 1) let's begin

The unit begins by asking students to revisit Lencho's faith and his attitude toward the post office officials.

The key skill is discussion: give an opinion and support it with story evidence.

## 2) sequencing

Students rearrange jumbled events from the story.

This checks plot understanding: need for rain, hailstorm, letter to God, postmaster's help, Lencho's reaction.

## 3) reading skill

Read for main idea, details, sequence, and inference.

While answering, underline keywords and avoid copying large chunks.

## 4) vocabulary

Learn words through context instead of memorising isolated meanings.

For this unit, useful fields include farming, weather, post office, faith, and help.

## 5) grammar and editing

Use grammar exercises to revise sentence clarity, tense consistency, connectors, and error correction.

Editing questions require careful reading word by word.

## 6) speaking and writing

Speaking tasks should use clear opinion language: "I think", "I agree because", "The story shows".

Writing tasks should be organised as beginning, middle, and conclusion.

## exam-friendly summary notes

- Sequence the story before writing answers.
- Use evidence for opinions about Lencho.
- Vocabulary must be understood in context.
- Editing needs grammar plus meaning.
- Writing should be clear and structured.

## common mistakes students make

1. Giving opinions without evidence.
2. Mixing the order of the hailstorm and letter.
3. Copying comprehension answers without understanding.
4. Ignoring punctuation in editing.

## board tips

- For workbook answers, keep language simple and exact.
- In discussions, mention both Lencho's faith and misunderstanding.
- In writing tasks, plan first, then draft.
