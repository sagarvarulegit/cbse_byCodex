# His First Flight: courage and family pressure

This lesson focuses on family pressure, the mother seagull, and the moment the young bird finally dives for food. That one action creates confidence.

## Story context

The young seagull has already been afraid for a long time. Now the family waits, the mother seagull holds back food, and the bird faces a choice.

The story shows that courage does not appear first as a feeling. It often begins as a small action under pressure.

## Key idea

The mother seagull does not give food immediately. She uses food to tempt him toward the edge, and the young bird must choose between fear and hunger.

The first dive matters because it breaks the fear pattern. After that, confidence grows quickly.

## Important moments

- The mother seagull comes close with food.
- The young seagull watches the food carefully.
- He feels the pressure of waiting and the fear of jumping.
- He makes the first dive.
- Once he acts, confidence starts to build.

## Character or speaker insight

The mother is firm and patient. Her action is not cruel; it forces the young seagull to face the real world.

The young seagull changes from anxious to confident because he discovers that he can survive the jump and fly.

## Solved examples

### Example 1

Question: Why does the mother seagull bring food close?

Answer: She wants the young seagull to move and try flying.

### Example 2

Question: What is the effect of the first dive?

Answer: It breaks fear and starts confidence.

### Example 3

Question: What changes after action begins?

Answer: The seagull feels stronger, braver, and more confident.

## Common mistakes

1. Saying the mother is simply being mean.
2. Forgetting the role of food.
3. Missing the first dive.
4. Writing that confidence comes before action.
5. Ignoring the change after the jump.

## Board tips

- Mention mother, food, dive, and confidence in the same answer.
- Write the cause first and the result next.
- Use short lines if the question is worth 2 or 3 marks.
- Keep the sequence of events correct.
