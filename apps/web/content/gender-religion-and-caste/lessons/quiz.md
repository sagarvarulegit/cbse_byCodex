# Gender, Religion and Caste chapter quiz

Answer these quick questions after revising the chapter notes.

## 1. Gender division

Gender division is mainly:

1. socially created roles for men and women
2. only a law of nature
3. a type of farming

Answer: 1
Explanation: Gender division is created by society, not by nature alone.

## 2. Representation

Women's representation matters because:

1. decisions should reflect women's experiences
2. democracy needs fewer voices
3. elections should stop

Answer: 1
Explanation: Women's representation helps decision-making reflect women's experiences and needs.

## 3. Communalism

Communalism uses religion to:

1. divide people politically
2. improve equality always
3. explain rainfall

Answer: 1
Explanation: Communalism turns religion into a political divide.

## 4. Secular state

India is secular because it:

1. has no official religion
2. bans all personal belief
3. supports only one religion

Answer: 1
Explanation: India does not have an official state religion.

## 5. Caste and politics

Caste affects politics, but:

1. it alone does not decide every election
2. it has no relation anywhere
3. it removes all other factors

Answer: 1
Explanation: Caste matters, but elections are shaped by many factors, not caste alone.
