## NCERT Chapter 12 Formulas to Cover

### Surface Area formulas:
- CSA of cylinder = 2πrh
- TSA of cylinder = 2πr(r+h)
- CSA of cone = πrl
- TSA of cone = πr(r+l)
- CSA of sphere = 4πr²
- CSA of hemisphere = 2πr²
- TSA of hemisphere = 3πr²

### Volume formulas:
- Volume of cylinder = πr²h
- Volume of cone = (1/3)πr²h
- Volume of sphere = (4/3)πr³
- Volume of hemisphere = (2/3)πr³

### Combination formulas:
- Volume of combination = sum of individual volumes (no hidden faces)
- Surface area of combination = sum of visible surfaces only (joined faces hide)

### Conversion:
- V(old shape) = V(new shape)

### Frustum:
- Volume = (1/3)πh(r₁² + r₂² + r₁r₂)
- CSA = π(r₁+r₂)l
- TSA = π(r₁+r₂)l + πr₁² + πr₂²

## Tips & Tricks to Include

1. **Cylinder formula pattern**: CSA = 2πrh (circumference × height). TSA = CSA + 2 × base = 2πrh + 2πr² = 2πr(h+r)
2. **Cone formula pattern**: CSA = πrl (half circumference × slant). TSA = CSA + base = πrl + πr² = πr(l+r)
3. **Sphere is simple**: Just 4πr² for everything (CSA = TSA = 4πr²)
4. **Hemisphere trick**: CSA = 2πr² (half of sphere). TSA = 3πr² (add one flat circle)
5. **Cone volume**: It's exactly 1/3 of a cylinder with same base and height
6. **Combination SA trap**: Don't add both TSAs — joined faces are invisible
7. **Combination volume is easy**: Just add volumes, no hidden faces
8. **Conversion golden rule**: Volume never changes when melting/recasting
9. **Frustum memory aid**: "Big cone minus small cone" — the formula is (1/3)πh(r₁² + r₂² + r₁r₂)
10. **r₁ vs r₂**: r₁ is the smaller radius (top cut), r₂ is the bigger radius (bottom base)
