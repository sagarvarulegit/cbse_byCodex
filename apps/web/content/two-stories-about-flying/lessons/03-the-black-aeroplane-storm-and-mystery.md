# The Black Aeroplane: storm and mystery

The pilot is flying to England when the weather changes fast. The storm, the instruments, and the danger create strong suspense.

## Learning goals

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:
- describe the pilot's journey to England
- explain how the storm creates suspense
- name the problem with the instruments
- retell the danger in simple English
- answer board questions about the event sequence

## Story context

The pilot wants to reach England and is confident at first. Then dark storm clouds appear, the flight becomes risky, and the instruments stop helping properly.

The tension keeps increasing because the pilot must make choices while the sky becomes unsafe.

## Key idea

The story is built around suspense. We know danger is near, but we do not know exactly how the pilot will escape it.

The broken instruments and the storm make the flight frightening and uncertain.

## Important moments

- The pilot flies toward England.
- Storm clouds gather around the plane.
- The flight becomes harder to control.
- The instruments fail to guide him properly.
- Suspense grows as danger closes in.

## Character or speaker insight

The pilot stays focused and brave even when the situation is dangerous. He keeps trying to fly safely.

The storm acts like a force that tests skill, patience, and calm thinking.

## Solved examples

### Example 1

Question: Why does the story feel tense?

Answer: The storm creates danger and suspense.

### Example 2

Question: What is the problem with the instruments?

Answer: They do not help the pilot guide the plane clearly.

### Example 3

Question: Why is England important here?

Answer: It is the destination the pilot wants to reach.

## Common mistakes

1. Ignoring the storm.
2. Forgetting the flight is to England.
3. Saying the pilot is safe all the time.
4. Missing the role of the instruments.
5. Underplaying the suspense.

## Board tips

- Name the destination first.
- Show how the storm changes the mood.
- Mention the instruments if the question asks about difficulty.
- Use suspense as a key word when describing tone.

## Quick practice

1. Where is the pilot flying?
2. What changes the mood of the story?
3. Why are the instruments important?
4. How does suspense grow?
5. What danger does the pilot face?

## Answer check

1. He is flying to England.
2. The storm changes the mood and creates danger.
3. They are important because they help guide the plane.
4. Suspense grows because the pilot is trapped in bad weather and unsure of escape.
5. He faces the danger of losing control in the storm.
